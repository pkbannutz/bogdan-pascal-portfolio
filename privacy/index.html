Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you visit bogdanpascal.com and how you can choose whether optional website analytics are used.

Your choice comes first. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are optional and have separate consent choices. Neither is intentionally loaded by our site's tracking code before you accept it. You can change your selection using the Cookie settings link in the website footer.

1. Who operates this website?

This professional portfolio is operated by Bogdan Pascal. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@bogdanpascal.com.

2. Basic website operation

The website is published using GitHub Pages. Technical information, including visitor IP addresses, can be processed by GitHub for delivering and securing the website, independently of optional analytics consent. Please see the GitHub privacy statement.

Some pages use fonts served by Google or include third-party media, links or YouTube embeds. Loading those resources may cause your browser to communicate directly with those services, which have their own privacy practices.

3. Google Analytics 4 (optional)

If you accept Google Analytics, we use it to understand visits, traffic sources and how visitors interact with pages. This may include page views, navigation and service clicks, contact-link clicks, section views, scrolling and compatible video events.

Google Analytics can process browser and device information, approximate geographical location, visited pages and cookie-based identifiers. We use its reports to understand and improve the portfolio. Clicking an email link is measured as an expression of interest, not as proof that an enquiry was sent.

The site uses the Google Analytics measurement ID G-X789N5N0QX. See Google's privacy policy for information about Google's processing.

4. Microsoft Clarity (optional)

If you separately accept Microsoft Clarity, it helps us understand how visitors use the website through heatmaps, scroll patterns and session replays. A session replay is a reconstruction of website activity such as scrolling, clicking and mouse movement, rather than a webcam recording or a video of your entire computer screen.

Clarity may process technical device information, browser information, interaction events and identifiers to connect page views in a session. We have enabled masking for form inputs and editable fields, and Clarity also provides masking settings. Please avoid entering sensitive information into public website controls.

Our Clarity project ID is zfp1d5boaq. See Microsoft's privacy statement and Clarity documentation.

5. Cookies and your preferences

Optional analytics and recording technologies are loaded by our website only after the relevant choice is accepted. The two choices are independent: you can allow Google Analytics without allowing Microsoft Clarity, or the reverse.

  • Google Analytics: may create cookies such as _ga and related identifiers when enabled.
  • Microsoft Clarity: may create cookies including _clck and _clsk when enabled.
  • Preference storage: your browser stores your answers locally under bp_analytics_consent_v1 and bp_clarity_consent_v1 so the site remembers your choices.

Use Cookie settings in the website footer, or the button above, to review, change or withdraw permission. Choosing to decline does not stop you browsing the portfolio. Existing browser privacy settings or content blockers may also limit tracking. Withdrawing consent stops new optional tracking, but does not automatically erase data collected earlier.

6. Retention and where information is stored

Analytics results are processed and stored in the providers' services, not in the public GitHub repository. Google Analytics user-level and event-level retention is controlled through the GA4 property's data retention settings, which support shorter or longer periods depending on the setting and account type.

Microsoft Clarity generally makes ordinary session replay data available for 30 days; some selected or favourited recordings may be kept for longer, up to nine months. Heatmap data is typically available for nine months under Microsoft's published retention policy. Provider retention policies can change.

These external services may process information outside your country. Their own published privacy and transfer arrangements apply. We do not use Clarity recordings to collect the contents of private messages or contact forms.

7. Contacting us

Clicking an email link may open your email application. If you send an email to hello@bogdanpascal.com, the details you voluntarily provide will be used to read and respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, discuss potential professional work. Emails are handled by the email provider separately from website analytics. Keep sensitive personal information out of initial enquiries where possible.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to ask for access to personal information, correction or deletion, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent. For questions or requests, email hello@bogdanpascal.com. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

9. Updates

We may update this policy when our website features, analytics providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest published revision.

# Athlete portfolio: Industry naming, demand, trends and SEO decision report

**Research date:** 18 November 2026  
**Website:** https://bogdanpascal.com/athlete/  
**Primary research market:** United Kingdom, English-language Google searches  
**Status:** **FIRST FULL SERVICE-FAMILY RESEARCH CHAPTER, RECOMMENDATIONS ONLY; NOTHING CHANGED ON THE WEBSITE.**  
**First read:** [RESEARCH-DECISION-PRINCIPLES.md](RESEARCH-DECISION-PRINCIPLES.md), which explains the user's underlying goal and explicitly instructs future AI researchers to challenge tactics that the evidence does not support.

## Executive decision

**Promote Bogdan as an actively hireable parkour athlete and freerunner who is also a screen stunt performer, specialist parkour coordinator and movement director.** Preserve the evidence, reel, role credentials and detailed capabilities. Do not make the entire page solely about coordination; do not call yourself a generic "extreme athlete" in primary headings; and do **not** relabel specialist coordination as a fully responsible `Stunt Coordinator` without verification of that production role and the accompanying responsibilities/registration.

**Industry-standard service names matter more than an artificial exact-keyword contest.** The best public-facing name is recognisable to casting, producers and agency clients; keyword alternatives are natural phrases in title/description, headings, body and evidence, not a collection of duplicate landing pages.

## 1. Research method: why the user-suggested approach needed adjustment

### Evidence layers completed
1. **Real site audit:** Inspected published athlete HTML/source, hero, navigation, credit proof, parkour coordinator section, performer skills and contact prompts.
2. **Industry-language verification:** Looked at professional Equity and One Dance UK role definitions, British Stunt Register categories, real parkour specialists and performer/talent agencies.
3. **Keyword demand:** Used DataForSEO / Google Ads search-volume dataset for **76 UK English search terms**, including a **12-month historical series from November 2025 to October 2026**. Exported full dataset to [ATHLETE-KEYWORD-METRICS-76.csv](keyword-research/ATHLETE-KEYWORD-METRICS-76.csv).
4. **Organic competition:** Queried DataForSEO's keyword-difficulty endpoint. Where no difficulty was returned, marked it unknown, not zero.
5. **Search intent:** Queried DataForSEO's intent classifier. These classifications are probabilistic and are **not** replacements for reading actual search pages.
6. **Google Trends:** Obtained UK search-term series for **2004-present**, **past five years**, **past 12 months**, and **past three months**, plus related queries. Used trends to challenge assumptions about growth and unrelated results.
7. **SERP checks:** Requested ten UK Google organic queries through a SERP data provider; some result names were irrelevant and destination URLs obscured by Google redirect wrappers. Therefore these are **not used to assert trustworthy top-ten competitor rankings**. Separately examined actual public industry/competitor websites with verifiable links.
8. **Gap analysis:** Checked whether names in current hero and sections reflect active athlete bookings alongside higher-responsibility movement roles.

### Critical distinctions
- **Google Trends 0–100 is RELATIVE search interest**, not monthly searches. A zero for niche `parkour performer` cannot prove zero bookings.
- **Google Ads keyword volume is an estimate**, generally including close variants, not an exact count of distinct interested clients.
- **Ads competition = competition among advertisers**, not organic search difficulty. Organic KD measures are third-party estimates, also not guarantees.
- A search spike might be a **game, software, TV show or viral topic**, not demand for professional parkour performers.
- A search result's high rank does **not** prove its vocabulary caused that rank. Authority, age, links, location, content and other factors matter.
- The 76 phrases are not 76 different offerings. They are overlapping ways people may describe a much smaller number of real jobs.

## 4. Trends: historical, durable and recent

### Long-term: Google Trends, UK, 2004–November 2026
- Generic **`parkour`** has the clearest long-term recognition; Trends sampled high relative interest in its early years and lower long-term scaled relative interest in the later years. **Caution:** this series is normalised against the 2004–2026 maximum, so a modern 5–6 reading does **not** mean only 5–6 searches.
- Generic **`freerunning`** is consistently much smaller than parkour in that same comparison.
- **`stunts`**, **`acrobatics`** and **`extreme sports`** persist, but represent broad interest rather than clients seeking production talent.
- Use this view for **historical context only**, not to choose page title wording or to extrapolate precise future demand.

### Durable trend: last 5 years, UK
- In the 5-year comparison with `freerunning`, `stunts`, `acrobatics` and `extreme sports`, `parkour` retains the strongest relative interest. Approximate sample index across 2022–2025 remains in the **54–62** range, and around **55** for 2026-to-date. This is broadly **stable**, not evidence of a sudden mass revival in hiring.
- `stunts` averaged roughly **21–25** by year in that same comparison, while `freerunning` is too small for useful Trends precision.
- Do **not** compare those index scores directly with the all-time graph above, since each query is separately normalised.

### 12 months: Nov 2025–Oct 2026, Google Ads estimated monthly UK searches
- `parkour`: monthly estimates mostly **14,800–18,100**, rising to **22,200 in August 2026**.
- `freerunning`: most months around **110–320**, with an exceptional **2,400 in August 2026**; the almost identical figures for `free running` and `freerunner` suggest **close-variant grouping and/or an external-event anomaly**. **Do not** conclude that hiring demand multiplied in August.
- `stunt performer`: often around **110–210**, with **390 in November 2025**.
- `movement director`: often around **40–110**, with **260 in October 2026**.
- `stunt coordinator`: generally around **90–140**, relatively stable.
- These are estimated **searches**, not direct casting enquiries, seasons of production, or conversion data.

### Recent: last 3 months, UK Trends
- Broad `parkour` remains more searched in relative terms than the other generic disciplines in the comparison; spike-prone day-to-day signals are **not** proof that one service should be renamed immediately.
- Related `freerunning` searches surfaced **`mtg freerunning`**, a game-related query, and `acrobat` related searches prominently surfaced **`acrobat adobe`, `acrobat pdf`**. These validate why unfiltered raw Trends rankings would be misleading.
- For the niche professional phrases `parkour coordinator`, `parkour performer`, `stunt performer` and `action choreographer`, multiple Trends comparisons showed **0 or sparse datapoints**. These are **insufficient measurement**, not proof that the services are unwanted.

**What to use for decisions:**
1. Historical all-time: identify whether a term is culturally durable or a fad, **without chasing peaks**.
2. Five-year: stable category recognition.
3. Twelve-month: recent direction and seasonality.
4. Three-month: new spikes, then **investigate root cause**.
5. **Actual monthly search estimates + relevant SERPs + hiring intent:** determine final industry service title.
6. **Your own GSC and enquiries:** override preliminary external models after real data exists.

## 6. What the current athlete page already does well and what it misses

**Observed in `athlete/index.html`:**
- Current title: `Bogdan Pascal | Parkour Coordinator · Stunt Performer · Movement Director`.
- Current hero roles: `PARKOUR COORDINATOR · STUNT PERFORMER · MOVEMENT DIRECTOR`.
- Current name H1: `BOGDAN PASCAL.`.
- The specialty `PARKOUR COORDINATOR` section has an actual explanation and **DISCUSS YOUR SEQUENCE** contact CTA.
- Skills list currently includes `Parkour / Freerunning`, `Stunt Performance`, `Acrobatics / Tumbling`, `Gymnastics`, `High Falls / Airbag`, `Trampoline`, `Tricking`, acting, martial arts, etc.
- Portfolio contains individual credits and visual evidence, with UK work eligibility and performer measurements.

**Opportunity 1:** The first-screen role line does not use **`Parkour Athlete`** or `Freerunner`, which are important to active performer/casting hireability.

**Opportunity 2:** Coordination has its own named section, but performer and acrobatic booking intents depend largely on the credits/skills list and generic `WORK WITH ME` CTA. A casting director might not immediately see the correct route to hire you *as a performer*.

**Opportunity 3:** A large literal list of physical skills is useful for casting, but those skill names are **not necessarily separate services**. Distinguish "capable of doing" from "available to coordinate/teach/consult".

**Opportunity 4:** The site should connect proof to specific roles (e.g., stunt and parkour work on Wicked, stage performance, live events) while **not asserting higher roles for credits that list you as performer/team member**.

### A. Public-facing hierarchy

**Primary visitor-facing identity:**  
`PARKOUR ATHLETE · STUNT PERFORMER · PARKOUR COORDINATOR`

**Supporting second-line identity:**  
`Freerunning, acrobatics, movement direction and action choreography for film, commercials and live performance.`

Rationale: the hero visibly says **active athlete**, preserves the recognised **Stunt Performer** role and still highlights your niche **Parkour Coordinator** specialty. `Movement Director` appears in a readable, high-position supporting line and in the established coordination section. An alternative if three role labels feel too long is `PARKOUR ATHLETE · STUNT PERFORMER · MOVEMENT DIRECTOR` with `Parkour Coordination` immediately below, not lost.

**Title:** `Bogdan Pascal | Parkour Athlete, Stunt Performer & Coordinator`

Keep the exact description natural even if Google rewrites its displayed snippet.

### D. First-pass keyword-to-content mapping

- **Hero / title:** parkour athlete; stunt performer; parkour coordinator.
- **First descriptive paragraph:** freerunner; parkour performer; movement director; stunt work in film/TV/commercials.
- **Coordination section:** parkour coordination; action movement choreography; movement direction; specialist talent where confirmed; preproduction and rehearsals.
- **Skills:** acrobatics, tumbling, tricking, gymnastics, high falls; descriptive *capabilities*, not dozens of "service" pages.
- **Live/show credits:** parkour live performance, freerunning display, brand activations, international events if offered and evidenced.
- **Contact:** service-aware CTA such as `Book me as a performer` and `Discuss parkour coordination`, preferably both via the same simple email pathway.

## 8. Recommendations ordered by effect, not SEO vanity

1. **P0, VISIBLE:** Fix positioning so a casting director immediately sees an **active Parkour Athlete & Stunt Performer**, not only a coordinator. Present exact hero copy for review.
2. **P0, SEARCH:** Update title and description to reflect active performer + coordination. No visitor-facing page layout change.
3. **P0, VISIBLE:** Differentiate `Hire as Performer` from `Discuss Parkour Sequence` with one or two uncluttered CTAs, respecting current design.
4. **P1, BACKEND:** Verify canonical, indexing, sitemap and valid metadata through existing technical audit checklist.
5. **P1, VISIBLE:** Enrich current specialist section with 2–4 concrete service actions rather than generic phrases, only where factual.
6. **P1, MEASUREMENT:** Compare Google Search Console searches and landing pages, then revise priorities against real data and qualified enquiries.
7. **P2, SEARCH/VISIBLE:** Use informative alt/caption text on selected relevant stunt/acrobat credits; do not stuff keywords into decorative images.
8. **P2, DISCOVERY:** Explore further Romanian/European market language only if the target hiring markets warrant dedicated research. **Do not reuse UK volumes for Romania**.
9. **P2, CONTENT:** Consider separate coordination case study only if new distinct, nonduplicate supporting evidence exists.
10. **DO NOT:** Rename specialist `Parkour Coordinator` to `Stunt Coordinator` as an SEO shortcut; use `Acrobat` without disambiguation; copy the busiest competitor's text; add keyword doorway pages; present trends as absolute monthly searches.

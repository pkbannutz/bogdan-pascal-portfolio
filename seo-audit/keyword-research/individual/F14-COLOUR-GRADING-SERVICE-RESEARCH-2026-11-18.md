## 0. Recommendation at a glance

A film client hires a colour grade to make the final imagery **balanced, consistent, deliberate and aligned with the story**, whether on an ad, music video, branded film or documentary. A client hires a **Colourist** to *perform* that work. These are **the same core craft expressed as result versus person**, so **F14 and F15 should share one substantive page**: existing **/filmmaker/services/colour-grading/**. They receive separate research because their search terms, supplier comparison and professional vocabulary differ.

**Best lead service wording:** **Colour Grading for Film & Commercials**.  
**Best creative descriptor:** **Colour Grading & Look Development**.  
**Best role beside it:** **Freelance Film Colourist**, using truthful current availability.  
**Avoid:** replacing the service with broad **Colour Correction** despite its vastly larger public search interest, because broad results involve software tutorials, photographs, beauty/print and colour settings, not necessarily a hired film professional.

**Most valuable next improvement:** real **before/after** or graded-shot comparison with project proof, **only if original neutral/untreated source stills are actually available and rights allow**. The current page has finished-frame comparisons across shots, which are useful, but they are **not before/after proof**. Don't invent a desaturated "before" frame or portray a LUT preset as the actual colourist workflow.

## 1. Professional craft scope and what commercial buyers purchase

[ScreenSkills Colourist](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/post-production/colourist/) treats grading as a distinct film post-production craft. [ScreenSkills Colourist Skills](https://www.screenskills.com/skills-checklists/unscripted-tv/post-production-department/colourist-skills/) covers technical and creative responsibility, matching and legal colour limits. A grade can include technical correction but is not synonymous with every finishing/delivery service in a post facility.

| Buyer | Actual deliverable and priority | F14 legitimate description | Boundary requiring proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising agency / commercial producer | Balanced multi-shot branded film with approved campaign look | Primary exposure/white-balance correction, skin tone, contrast, creative look, shot matching | Complete mastering across every SDR/HDR/broadcast spec, colourist-certified room automatically |
| Director / DoP | Image should respect intention shot-to-shot | Collaboratively develop colour palette and final visual continuity | Automatic personal DoP/camera credit on same production |
| Beauty/fashion producer | Premium restrained skin/product colour, consistency | Natural skin, highlight control and subject/palette separation where footage allows | Guaranteed product colour accuracy without calibrated capture/monitor and reference checks |
| Music-video director | More stylised creative look | Film/music video grading, versioning as agreed | VFX cleanup, roto/compositing, commissioned LUT/PowerGrade delivery by default |
| Documentary/independent film producer | Calm natural look across mixed cameras/lighting | Matching, exposure decisions and story-led visual continuity | True scene reconstruction from impossible source data, HDR theatrical mastering |
| Editor/post-producer | Handoff from picture lock to grade | Working via agreed files, timelines, conform/project media where supported | Separate online editor, DIT, VFX and mastering supervisor skills automatically included |

**Distinguish terms:** `Colour Correction` is technical repair/balance and frequently part of grading. `Colour Grading` includes creative shaping and continuity. `Look Development` expresses the creative planning/creation, not always a separately priced occupation. `Film Colourist` is the professional role, F15. `DaVinci Resolve Colourist` is software-qualified descriptive language, not a formal certification. Some UK clients type **color grading**, US spelling; that doesn't justify duplicate pages or false location keywords.

**Technical scope to verify by job:** camera colour space and log transforms, conform and relinking, source resolution and bit depth, reference display/room calibration, Rec.709/HDR format, client approval viewing chain, legal signal levels and final delivery handoff. **A grading service doesn't by itself prove a broadcast-certified suite or Dolby Vision delivery**.

# F01 / 30: Commercial Filmmaker

**Individual report date:** 18 November 2026.  
**Search market:** UK-English qualified hiring for commercial and branded films, not generic consumer film watching.  
**Decision:** **CORE service and authentic professional identity.** Best personal title: **Freelance Commercial Filmmaker** or **Commercial Filmmaker & Cinematographer**; best **client-outcome service**: **Branded Films & Commercial Video Production**, scoped to confirmed personal production capacity.  
**Status:** RESEARCH COMPLETE; no public website, SEO, code or media changes.

## 0. The one-minute recommendation

**The distinction matters:** F01 is the client buying **a branded story, creative video or commercial deliverable**, or engaging one filmmaker to carry visual decisions across several stages. F02 Director of Photography is the producer buying **senior visual/camera leadership on a production**, which may or may not involve delivering the final film. F03 Cinematographer is substantially the **same senior camera profession as F02**, not a new separate craft to advertise through duplicated pages.

Keep F01 prominently visible as **Commercial Filmmaker**, with natural `branded films, commercials and selected documentary/music videos` language and a well-scoped enquiry pathway. Preserve `Director of Photography / Cinematographer` as a separately linked crew-hire category. Do not call Bogdan an advertising agency, executive producer, production company with permanent crew or commercial director of every film.

**Proposed landing surface:** existing `/filmmaker/` is already the creative portfolio with 23 projects, ideal for F01 overarching identity. `/filmmaker/services/` currently offers five specialist stages, and a precise `COMMERCIAL & BRANDED FILMS` intro or linked anchor could bridge the gap. Do NOT create a second generic commercial filmmaker page just to repeat existing portfolio and DoP service.

## 1. Exact F01 buyer needs and deliverable boundaries

| Buyer | What they buy | F01 can realistically discuss | What should not be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand marketing team | Branded narrative/product campaign, content suite or commercial | Creative development, filming and finished edits within agreed actual scope | Complete full-service agency, media buying or creative strategy guarantee |
| Advertising agency / producer | Individual director/camera/editor for a defined stage or compact film | Filmmaking role, own kit, shot-led production and post-production if booked | Full crew/line producer, casting, talent rights, specialist permissions by default |
| Small business/entrepreneur | High-quality campaign video with clear deliverable | Small crew, production+edit package **only when real availability/costs established** | Arbitrary film budgeting or TV-cleared broadcast standards automatically |
| Documentary branded-content commissioner | Authentic brand story and interviews/visual narrative | Cinematography and storytelling, documentary-style production where shown | Being sole director or producing company for every `Therme`/BBC-branded film |
| Music artist/label | Visual music content | Project-specific cinematography/filmmaker involvement and editing if contracted | Automatically `music video director` or producer on all existing credits |

The scope of F01 is intentionally **not** F19/F20 research; future Music Video Filmmaking and Independent Film Production each get independent reports. One person's ability to shoot and edit does not prove all line-production/contractor/copyright responsibilities or unlimited project capacity.

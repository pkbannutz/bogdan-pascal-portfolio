# P03 / 30: Parkour Coordinator / Consultant

**Completed:** 18 November 2026  
**Scope:** Individual professional service research, only P03. **Status: RESEARCH DONE, WEBSITE NOT EDITED.**  
**Market:** UK-English film, TV, advertising, music video, branded content and specialist live production, with international work in scope.  
**Verdict:** **CORE DISTINCT SPECIALIST SERVICE**, not just another way of saying parkour athlete or stunt coordinator. **Preferred label: Parkour Coordinator. Supporting commercial label: Parkour Consultant.**

## 1. Executive decision

**Rank 1 industry/client-facing name:** **Parkour Coordinator**.  
**Rank 2 buyer-search-oriented alternative:** **Parkour Coordinator & Consultant**.  
**Rank 3 explanatory offer:** **Parkour Sequence Design & Movement Consulting**.

The service offers specialist parkour action planning, creative movement development, feasible location/route advice, performance coaching and collaboration with camera and stunt departments. Producers can hire a specialist for the parkour component without transferring **production-wide stunt department leadership, overall stunt risk assessments, insurance approvals or responsibility for unrelated hazardous work**. This is an essential boundary with P05.

**Commercial architecture:** maintain a high-visibility Parkour Coordinator section on the existing athlete page, possibly with a distinct P03 enquiry subject and demonstrable planning case studies. Consider a separate page only if the job can be substantiated with enough unique personal coordination credits, a clearly different buyer task and actual organic/enquiry evidence. Do **not** create an automatic SEO doorway page.

**Most important honesty issue:** the live-facing site already advertises extensive P03 capabilities but does not tie them to multiple clearly identified **personally commissioned Parkour Coordinator credits**, making it hard to distinguish what was independently led, done as a team member or simply could be offered. The credible approach is to keep specialist positioning but add verifiable, scoped production examples, not broaden into P05.

## 2. Buyer, deliverables and boundaries

| Buyer | Real request | P03 can offer subject to experience/project agreement | Do not silently promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Director / creative director | Parkour chase or expressive movement fitted to story and shot | Concept breakdown, movement beats, rehearsable movement options, collaboration with DoP | Directing/owning the entire film action department |
| Producer | Reliable specialist advice, budget/time feasibility, athletes and location checks | Specialist consultation, artist sourcing where personally arranged, expert input and scheduling | Registered casting agency, licensed H&S professional or overall insurance compliance |
| Stunt coordinator / 2nd unit | Parkour-specific execution and movement design | Detailed parkour consultation, feasibility, rehearsal, physical feedback | Replacing supervising stunt coordinator or independently signing full risk assessments |
| Agency / brand production | Movement for a commercial, live campaign or music video | Movement choreography, coach-athlete coordination, camera-led timing | Guarantee of every trick/height; production-wide stunt supervision |
| Events/live production | Specialty parkour staging and talent | Movement flow, performer rehearsal and stage blocking | Venue structural approval, rigging signoff, all event safety paperwork |

**Distinction from adjacent original categories:** P01 books the **person performing**; P02 books a **freerunning movement aesthetic**; P03 books **specialist planning and coordination**. P04 is screen-stunt physical performance. P05 is the **larger stunt coordinator role and responsibilities**. P07 Movement Director and P08 Action Choreographer should each get their own independent analysis later; they can be named supporting P03 activities but are not automatically synonyms.

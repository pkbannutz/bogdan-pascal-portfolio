## 0. Decision in one minute

**Best official visitor-facing service name:** **Parkour Athlete & Performer**.

**Best search / hiring phrase to naturally support:** **Professional Parkour Performer for Film, TV & Commercials**.

**Best format:** a highly visible **Parkour Performance / Book Me as a Performer** pathway on the existing personal athlete portfolio. The current page already carries an individual casting identity, a showreel, production highlights, measurements, and contact. Give active performance at least equal prominence to coordination; do not create an SEO duplicate just to claim a 31st URL.

**Why:** `parkour athlete` communicates the authentic, active practice. `parkour performer` communicates bookable on-camera services. Real UK talent agents and independent professionals deliberately use both, not just `parkour coordinator` or `stunt coordinator`. A person looking for someone to execute parkour on screen is a different customer from someone recruiting the head of action/stunt planning. Keep role boundaries truthful and separately research P02-P10.

**Priority judgment:** **very high business fit, strong first-party performer proof, narrow organic search demand, high value per qualified enquiry; approval-gated on-page opportunity.** This is an editorial prioritization, not a measured ROI calculation or predicted rank.

## 1. Exact scope of P01

**What P01 is:** direct professional booking of Bogdan as the athlete whose movement is captured or performed. Examples: a parkour sequence in a film, running/vaulting/jumping on a commercial set, a film/TV specialist movement performance, on-camera brand sport content or a camera-led athletic performance.

**What P01 is NOT:** a competing claim that he is automatically the overall stunt coordinator, a licensed trainer, certified event safety authority, parkour agency representing a roster, world champion, or a guarantee to accept every risk/location. These roles/claims require separate evidence. P01 must not absorb P02 Freerunning Performer, P03 Parkour Coordinator, P04 Stunt Performer, P05 Stunt Coordinator, P06 Acrobat, P07 Movement Director or P10 Live Show simply because these overlap in real careers. P01 can link to them when each gets its own review.

**Buyer groups and distinct jobs:**

| Buyer | Wants to book | Proof they inspect | Best P01 response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casting directors and production coordinators | Specific visible parkour athlete for film/TV | Head/body casting dimensions, up-to-date photos, experience, performance reel, availability | "Book as a parkour performer" and exact credits |
| Commercial producers / ad agencies | Athletic performance for a branded visual | Ad/screen footage, on-set professionalism, physical versatility, repeatable takes | 30-60 second movement example, project briefs, usage discussion |
| Directors, DPs and camera crews | Camera-aware action performer, repeatable action and timing | BTS/on-screen sequence footage, comfort with repeat takes, physical capabilities | Screen-action-specific reel and simple contact |
| Brands, editorial teams and photographers | Parkour athlete in campaign/social/editorial | Recent portrait/full body, movement portfolio, client history | Photo/video selection and usage / date / location details |

The `parkour athlete` term alone also attracts general fans, lessons, training and sport curiosity. Combining it with **performer** and **for film/TV/commercials** protects hiring intent.

| Five years | `parkour` remains visible in UK weekly normalized Trends panels, with roughly stable 2023-2026 annual panel means (~55 in the source study) | Subject recognition remains; no clear sustained collapse or explosive recent growth proved |
| Nov 2025 to Oct 2026 monthly Ads | Broad `parkour` often ~14,800-18,100 and Aug 2026 ~22,200; specialist `parkour athlete` fluctuates ~20-50; `parkour performer` frequently 0-10 in monthly rows | The hireable niche is small and noisy; do not call month-to-month fluctuations a market trend |

**Trends limitation:** Google Trends is a normalized relative index; zeros on extremely niche phrases are often sampling suppression, not zero searches. No validated **booking seasonality**, **2026 job revenue trend**, or **commercial conversion rate** exists.

### Search-facing metadata, suggested only

**Search title (recommended):**
```text

```

**Alternative, for heavier screen-stunt branding:**
```text

```text

Do not generate a second `/parkour-performer/` page that duplicates the personal portfolio without a real independent user need and unique substance.

# F19 / 30: Music Video Filmmaking

**Date:** 18 November 2026. **UK English hiring market:** record-label commissioners, music artists and management, music-video production companies, directors, agencies and DoPs looking for collaborators. **Status:** DONE as individual profession/SEO research; wider personally directed or fully produced music-video credits not established. **Public site:** UNCHANGED.

## 0. Executive decision

**Music Video Cinematography** is the most strongly evidenced personally bookable specialization within F19. **Music Video Filmmaking** is a wider project type, which can also include personally directed, shot and edited productions **when those exact roles are confirmed**. The best personal job title for current documented evidence is **Music Video Cinematographer / Director of Photography**. The client-facing broader project descriptor may say **Music Video Filming & Visual Production**, explicitly specifying whether Bogdan is booked as **DoP / camera operator**, **director** or an **end-to-end filmmaker** for each assignment.

**Why:** [Fauness: Inanimate Girl](../../../filmmaker/work/inanimate-girl/index.html) is explicitly a **music video** credited **Director: Anda Teglas** and **DoP: Bogdan Pascal**. This establishes music-video cinematography, **not** a Bogdan personal music-video directorship, music label contract, full budget producing job or editor credit. A performance appearance in another artist's film (e.g. 1975 movement/acrobat credit in the athlete portfolio, if verified) is **not** a behind-camera music video credit.

**Classification:** CORE **project specialization** of existing F02 DoP/F01 commercial filmmaker services, not automatically a separate sixth service URL. **Confidence:** high professional title and one directly observed credited music-video DoP case; medium additional personal music-video breadth; unknown personal director/producer/edit roles beyond credited projects; no measured completed music-video leads.

## 1. Three different paid briefs, not one interchangeable service

| Buyer | Actual offer being hired | Appropriate personal title | What must be verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Label's or artist's producer has a director and budget | Plan visual capture with director, lighting/camera strategy, crew collaboration | **Music Video DoP / Cinematographer** | Personal shot credit, director/producer collaboration |
| Music-video director or production company | Operate a camera, movement/gimbal or FPV during their shoot | **Music Video Camera Operator**; FPV/Gimbal where genuinely filmed | Camera/FPV specifics on the actual video |
| Independent artist wants one person for a modest shoot | Defined creative preproduction, shooting, edit and agreed finished deliverables | **Music Video Filmmaker** only **within a defined commission scope** | Who directed, produced, managed permissions, edited, graded, cleared music and delivered |
| Artist asks for creative direction/treatment | Idea, artist performance, storyboard, shots and on-set direction | **Music Video Director** only for projects actually directed by Bogdan | Named directing credit or personally commissioned directing reel |
| Manager wants a full-label-grade production partner | Director, casting, locations, permits, crew, production management, post and deliveries | **Production company** or producer-led team | A real company, team contracts, insurance, permissions, specific responsibilities |

**Actual role definitions:** [ScreenSkills Film/TV Director](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/development-film-and-tv-drama-job-profiles/director-film-and-tv-drama/) separates director responsibilities from the DoP's photography role. [In Color Music Videos](https://incolor.film/music-videos) presents a producer-led label/artist/director service, a different purchase from an individual DoP. [Music Video London crew roles](https://musicvideo.london/cost-of-making-music-video/) also separates producer, director, director of photography and post. Suppliers' rate pages are not a reliable personal quote or proof of current standardized UK rates.

# F18 / 30: Film Post-Production

**Researched:** 18 November 2026. **UK English market:** directors, agencies, brands, independent filmmakers, production companies seeking an editor, finisher or bounded picture-and-sound post workflow. **Status:** DONE as an individual research decision; multi-department end-to-end personal case proof still needs confirmation. **Site:** RESEARCH ONLY, NOT MODIFIED.

## 0. Executive conclusion

**Film Post-Production** is a real **commercial buyer need and umbrella production stage**, not simply a synonym of Film Editor. For Bogdan's current independently documented portfolio the best truthful public-facing service is **Video Editing & Post-Production**, qualified with **Picture Editing, Colour Grading & Sound Design**. Use `Post-Production for Film & Commercials` as an explanatory cross-discipline services section and organic search phrase, rather than implying a fully staffed `Film Post-Production Studio` or automatically building a sixth near-duplicate URL.

**Personal role:** `Editor / Colourist / Sound Designer` *for individually confirmed assignments only*, or `Filmmaker working across cinematography and post-production` where a project has only a credited DoP role. A professional `Post-Production Supervisor`, `Online Editor`, `Conform Artist`, `DI Producer` and `Post Facility` each carry different production/technical responsibilities, not all established by DaVinci Resolve proficiency or an extensive filmmaking portfolio.

**Classification:** CORE umbrella **service pathway** with a serious buyer-intent opportunity; operationally split across existing F12/F13, F14/F15 and F16/F17 service pages. **Confidence:** high service naming / high multi-service website presence / medium documented individual end-to-end commercial delivery / low full-facility and supervision claims.

[ScreenSkills film/TV Editor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/post-production/editor-film-and-tv-drama/) describes collaborative assembly, director's cut and picture lock. [ScreenSkills Online Editor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/post-production/grade-and-online-suite/online-editor-post-production/) identifies a distinct senior finishing role after offline editorial; [ScreenSkills Post-Production Supervisor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/post-production/management/post-production-supervisor-post-production) works across picture and sound departments, schedule, coordination and deliverables. One person editing and colour-grading footage is **not automatically a credited post supervisor**.

**Workflow distinction:** `picture edit` -> `picture lock / approved cut` -> `conform or media relink where necessary` -> `colour and sound work` -> `master / cutdowns / deliverable checks`. This is a conceptual sequence, not a claim the same freelancer executed every step or that every client project is linear.

- [ScreenSkills Online Editor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/post-production/grade-and-online-suite/online-editor-post-production/) and [Post-Production Supervisor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/post-production/management/post-production-supervisor-post-production) provide job boundaries, not personal eligibility.
- [The Farm Post](https://farmpost.tv/) is an actual **multi-department post facility** offering picture, colour, sound and workflows with a dedicated team. It is a **different supplier scale** from a solo freelancer; do not copy its claims.
- [Martin Cantwell](https://cantwell.co.uk/) illustrates the useful alternative: an individually bookable, strongly role-credited specialist rather than an anonymous studio.
- [Here Now Films finishing and delivery](https://www.herenow.film/london/post-production-london/finishing-and-delivery) represents buyer terminology for commercial picture finishing; exact scope must be checked before claiming equivalent technical pipeline.
- [ScreenSkills film/TV editor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/post-production/editor-film-and-tv-drama/) distinguishes narrative picture-editing work.
- [UK F12/F13 family original SERP archive](../editing-google-serp-raw-2026-11-18.json) has seven **partial** queries. The `film editing post production services UK` snapshot contains directories, post houses and service suppliers; they are not all personal freelance competitors, and Google redirect URLs do not establish certified final positions. **No fresh complete F18 top-ten UK desktop Google ranking was independently validated** in this research.

**What wins a client:** a precise personally attributable finished-work case, transparent scope, measurable delivery variants and easy contact. A solo professional competing on the phrase `full-service post-production company` is poorly positioned unless the infrastructure exists.

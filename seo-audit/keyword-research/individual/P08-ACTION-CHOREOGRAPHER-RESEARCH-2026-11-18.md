# P08 / 30: Action Choreographer

**Date:** 18 November 2026  
**Market:** UK film/TV, commercial and music-video production; distinction between physical *movement design*, fight choreography, dance choreography and stunt supervision.  
**Research status:** **DONE. WEBSITE NOT EDITED.** Only original category **P08** is assessed here.  
**Verdict:** **Legitimate creative service family, but original generic title `Action Choreographer` is broader than the demonstrated role evidence**. Preferred near-term specialty name: **Parkour Action Choreography & Movement Design**. **Personally credited full Action Choreographer assignments are NOT yet verified**.

## 1. Executive recommendation

**Best honest scope:** **Parkour Action Choreography** (script-to-movement design, traversals, vault lines, actor/athlete rehearsal and repeatable camera-safe physical beats). Pair it with **Action Movement Design** to signal cinematic collaboration, not general fight, weapons and every stunt discipline.

**Distinct buyer value:** A production can hire a specialist to **invent and rehearse a particular action sequence** without that person performing the shots (P01/P04), setting the entire physical language of a production (P07), or being the stunt department's final authority (P05). This is a genuine creative service, not an SEO synonym.

**What to avoid:** adopting the unqualified headline `Fight Choreographer`, `Action Director`, `Second Unit Director`, `Stunt Coordinator`, `Stunt Safety Supervisor` or `Fight Arranger` just to appear in more Google searches. Such roles need independently verifiable training, exact credited contracts and safety/legal authority. `Action Choreographer` may be offered only with suitably narrow explicit parkour/action-movement scope; do not imply expertise across close-combat, firearms, vehicle stunts or all fights.

**Recommended site:** A concise **`PARKOUR ACTION CHOREOGRAPHY`** capability within the existing P03 `Parkour Coordinator` section, with a **real case-study breakdown and sequence clips**. Do not immediately create a `/athlete/action-choreography/` URL with similar text to P03, P07 and P04.

## 2. Real distinction and typical buyer

| Buyer | P08 deliverable | Nearby role to keep distinct |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial/music-video director | Plan 5-30s visually legible parkour sequence, movement beats, action style, camera-aware transitions and rehearsal | **P07 movement director** can shape overall physical style, but this is actual designed sequence |
| Film director/DP/second unit | Storyboard-aware route, movement blocking, repetition and actor/athlete coaching | **P03 parkour coordinator** may oversee and advise larger parkour specialist process |
| Appointed stunt coordinator | Parkour-specific movement/choreography specialist and rehearsal support on assigned sequences | **P05 stunt coordinator** remains responsible for delegated stunt supervision and risk planning as contract requires |
| Casting/performance director | Explain required style and skills, adapt choreography to each performer's capabilities | **P04 stunt performer** executes stunt-classified action and needs own booking |
| Brand/live-event creative | Formatted movement sequence for launch, on-stage camera work or social video | **P10 live performance** is a different solo/team booking task |

**Attribution test:** Designing a single person's own trick sequence is valid **self-choreographed performance**, but is not automatically evidence of managing choreography for actors or multiple performers on a professional production. Distinguish **`self-choreographed`**, **`parkour movement consultant`**, **`action choreographer`**, **`fight choreographer`**, and **`stunt coordinator`** in project captions.

Large action studios may have producer/team/rigging resources that a one-person portfolio must not promise. Current discoverability of a few named sites is **not a validated ordered UK Google top-ten, competitor domain rating or proof of low competition**.

Archived UK web Trends panels: [long+3mo](../athlete-discovery-and-long-trends-2026-11-18.json), [5y+12mo](../athlete-data-raw-2026-11-18.json). Relative 0-100 scores are normalized **within panel and window**, never absolute demand or directly comparable cross-window.

- **12 months:** same role mix `action choreographer ~0` and `movement director ~38` in normalized group. **This is not zero monthly searches**, as separate Ads estimates give 140 for broad action choreography.
- **Recent three months:** only broad `parkour/stunts` terms captured; `stunts` same-panel mean ~20, but not direct choreography hiring demand.
- **Ads last twelve months:** the two `action choreographer` and `action choreography` variants show roughly **110-170 monthly** with same month-by-month history. No recurrent growth or seasonality from one year.

**Important:** highly specific film services get hired through producers, stunt teams and referrals; general web-search trends cannot proxy booked films or pay rates. No credible 2026 action choreography boom or seasonal peak can be claimed from these panels.

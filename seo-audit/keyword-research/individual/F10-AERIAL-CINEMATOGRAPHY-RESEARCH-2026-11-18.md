**Individual research date:** 18 November 2026. **Primary search context:** UK-English professional filmmaking, commercial, documentary, TV and branded production; remote cross-border flights separately permissioned.  
**Verdict:** **CORE distinct CINEMATIC VISUAL SERVICE and recognised professional descriptive identity**, often provided via the SAME drone operator/DoP who performs F09; **not an automatically separate operational licence, department grade or duplicate URL**.  
**Preferred audience label:** **Aerial Cinematography**; person when personally cinematographing the footage: **Drone Cinematographer / Aerial Camera Operator**.  
**Status:** RESEARCH COMPLETE, NO LIVE WEBSITE CODE, ASSET OR SEO CHANGES.

## 0. Executive recommendation

Where F09 answers **`who operates the drone legally for a production?`**, F10 answers **`who creates and photographs the elevated visual scene that tells our story?`**. Aerial cinematography spans visual language, composition, timing, lens/camera perspective, geography, atmosphere, smooth rises/orbits/reveals and production look consistency. The camera can be on a conventional stabilised drone, FPV, helicopter or specialist crane/remote aircraft, depending on crew and budget. **Bogdan's currently offered service evidence is drone-based**; do **not** imply he supplies a helicopter, heavy-lift camera unit, aircraft charter, specialist dual-op cinema drone or certified aerial camera head.

**Best combined first-screen words for real site:** `FPV DRONE PILOT · AERIAL CINEMATOGRAPHY`. Two different buyer tasks on one coherent `/filmmaker/services/fpv/` page, which already has conceptual `FPV` and `AERIAL` panels. F10 can be directly bookable within that page without building a second near-identical `/aerial-cinematographer/` landing.

**Most important commercial proof:** a true personally piloted/shot **conventional aerial** section distinct from FPV chase sequences, showing establishing shot, controlled reveal and geographic/scale visual intent; project and actual personal camera/pilot roles must be evidenced, not inferred from film artwork.

## 1. Buyer segmentation and cinematography versus drone operation

| Buyer | They want | Camera craft / F10 deliverable | Necessary boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Film/TV director / DoP | Establish geography, continuity or spectacle from above | Frame selection, scale and movement at the right time, camera direction, look and sequence coverage | Aircraft, legally authorised pilot, permissions and crew plan explicitly checked |
| Brand/commercial director | Hero reveal, property/large venue scale or visual scene transition | Cinematic aerial composition, movement rhythm matching campaign treatment | Not automatically a complete branded video/crew/grade |
| Documentary / tourism film | Landscape, architecture, movement through large spaces | Story-supporting overheads, wide/low orbits, scenic motion | Wildlife/remote sites airspace/nature law, no blanket access |
| Film production DoP | Existing visual language must match ground coverage | Technical/logistical aerial camera support and visual continuity | If a separate pilot/camera op is contracted, don't conflate their credits |
| Sports/parkour filmmaker | Geographic context of action, high/low transition and real speed | Complementary controlled aerial + action/FPV angles | Hazardous stunt/drone chase is F08/P05 collaborated work, not a normal F10 assumption |

**The service is an aesthetic/production result, not "all overhead filming."** A straight top-down inspection still may be `aerial photography`, not compelling cinema. F10 can include static aerial filming as the best directorial choice; an FPV race-like follow may instead serve F08. **`Drone Cinematographer` is a meaningful role only when film image-making decisions really undertaken**, while `Commercial Drone Operator` alone may only represent piloting/flight logistics.

**Aerial photography vs moving-image cinematography:** User's film portfolio predominantly supplies moving-image visual stories. Avoid ranking personal site primarily for `drone photography`, `roof survey`, `real estate stills` or engineering mapping without a real and separately evidenced offering. Large generic volumes in those categories would create unqualified contacts.

## 9. Legality and scope confirmation, concise but essential

Same regulatory aircraft exposure as F09/F08, even if creative goal differs. [UK CAA current 2026 requirements](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/open-category/moving-on-to-more-advanced-flying/flying-drones-for-work/) distinguish legal operator registration, remote pilot competence, third-party insurance and additional operational authorisation for Specific Category. [EASA](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/drones-air-mobility/operating-drone) uses risk-based open/specific/certified categories rather than a commercial-only pilot licence. **Do not market A2 CofC as universal filming authorization**, particularly over crowded/urban restricted locations, or infer UK/EU reciprocity for every approval.

**Stunt camera overlap:** An **aerial stunt shot** is **not automatically authorised as a normal F10 aerial shot**. It requires real stunt/aviation risk coordination; a drone cinematographer is not the same as a fully staffed stunt camera crew. Candidate longtails `aerial stunt camera crew`, `FPV stunt cinematography`, `drone stunt filming` remain **unknown volume**, not a reason for fake separate titles.

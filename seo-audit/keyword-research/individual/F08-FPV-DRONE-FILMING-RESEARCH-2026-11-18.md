**FPV is defined by control/view method, not one weight class**. Cinewhoop/micro, racing-style 5-inch and heavy-lift rigs have different mass, camera, risk and authorization. A `5-inch FPV pilot` is not necessarily an authorised heavy-lift cinema operator, and a 249 g claim cannot be inferred from propeller diameter.

## 7. Legal/operational reality, current 2026 UK and EU

**UK CAA sources, current 18 November 2026:**
- [CAA A2 CofC](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/specific-category/remote-pilot-qualifications/a2-certificate-of-competency-a2-cofc/) says A2 certificate relates to the **Near People (A2)** subcategory, **not universal drone filming authorisation**. UK regulations changed 1 January 2026; aircraft class/weight, operational category, flight zone, IDs and other permissions are decisive.
- [CAA Flying Drones for Work](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/open-category/moving-on-to-more-advanced-flying/flying-drones-for-work/) and [CAA Specific Category](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/specific-category/specific-category-overview) distinguish **open-category lower-risk commercial flights** and **specific-category operational authorisation**. Former `PfCO` permission was withdrawn when regulatory regime changed; don't demand or advertise it as mandatory current license.
- [CAA Remote Pilot Qualifications](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/specific-category/remote-pilot-qualifications/remote-pilot-qualifications-overview/) describes A2 and GVC applicability/transition.

**EU/EASA sources, current:**
- [EASA operational categories](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/drones-air-mobility/operating-drone): **open/specific/certified** categories are primarily risk based, **not a separate hobby vs commercial license**. Paying for a job **does not automatically move every open flight into specific**, and A2 alone **does not permit** restricted/high-risk work.
- [EASA FPV visual observer](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/the-agency/faqs/i-am-drone-racing-andor-flying-drones-goggles-fpv-open-category) requires appropriate direct visual observation by an unmanned-aircraft observer for FPV goggles where permitted in Open category.
- [EASA rules revised June 2026](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/online-publications/easy-access-rules-unmanned-aircraft-systems?erules-id=ERULES-1963177438-15551) and [national authority guidance](https://www.caa.ro/) govern country/geozones and current class-specific details. UK CAA ID/qualification and EASA operator registration cannot be assumed **mutually recognised** without national checking.

**FPV chase brief gate:** identify precise aircraft mass/class, remote pilot IDs, camera/payload, flight zone and airspace, people including uninvolved bystanders, planned path, spotter, production crowd/stunt safety controls, country and permissions/insurance. The pilot's A2 CofC cannot substitute for an operational authorisation when specific-category operations required. Never promise flying over uninvolved crowds, unsafe urban chases, BVLOS, beyond airspace limits or indoor staging without case-by-case assessment.

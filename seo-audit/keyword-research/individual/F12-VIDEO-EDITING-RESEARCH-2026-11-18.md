## 0. Executive verdict and precise business recommendation

**CORE, genuinely bookable professional service**, supported by an existing dedicated, unusually substantive editing webpage and an established 12-year filmmaking/editing background. Its *client outcome* is **Video Editing & Post-Production**. The strongest *professional hire identity* for primary commercial buyers is **Commercial Video Editor** (with **Freelance Video Editor** as a useful hiring phrase). For storytelling and director-led film projects use the **Film Editor** label, evaluated independently in F13.

**Recommended site architecture:** preserve and strengthen **one** current URL `/filmmaker/services/editing/`. F12 commercial editing and F13 film/narrative editing are **meaningfully different buyer/project briefs**, but both can honestly live on this one real editorial portfolio with differentiated, role-verified examples. Do **not** generate duplicate `/commercial-editor/`, `/video-editor/`, `/film-editor/`, `/social-video-editor/` pages containing the same film list. A separately detailed narrative case-study page may become useful once first-person edit credits and production stories are established.

**Commercial strategy:** producer or agency can book just editorial work on their own footage, or commission editing plus explicitly agreed colour/sound/format deliverables. Never imply the client must buy Bogdan's cinematography/drone work to hire him to edit. A simple **`HIRE A VIDEO EDITOR`** or **`DISCUSS YOUR EDIT`** CTA should clarify it.

## 1. Actual buyer jobs and editorial scope

| Buyer | Hiring question | Relevant F12 offering | Never silently claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial/brand producer | Who cuts our brand film from provided footage? | Selects, assembles and refines storytelling and pacing; rounds of agreed revisions | Full creative strategy, brand performance/sales outcome guaranteed |
| Agency / director | Need a freelance Editor to collaborate with existing team and deliver approved cut | Edit passes, feedback versions, music/graphics placeholders, conform/handoff | Licensed stock/music, paid plugin seats or legal clearances included |
| Social content/marketing team | Need master plus 9:16/1:1/4:5 short versions | Vertical reframing, shorter cutdowns and deliverable checks | Mass-volume influencer content factory or guaranteed views |
| Smaller producer | Needs edit + grade/audio coordinated | Personally offered editing with colour/sound collaboration only where commissioned and technically feasible | Full offline/online facility, Dolby Vision/HDR master or licensed broadcast deliverables automatically |
| Documentary/short film director | Story and performance rhythm matters more than social versioning | Creative narrative picture editing (F13 specific role) | Personal feature-film editing credits not verified in current project register |
| Client searching `free video editor` | Usually software/app | Not a freelance booking lead | No reason to target with client page |

**Professional distinction:** ScreenSkills and UK National Careers Service recognise editor/picture editor as a professional role. `Online Editor`, `Assistant Editor`, `Conform Editor`, `Motion Graphics Designer` and `Colourist` may be separate jobs depending on production scale. This F12 **does not grant every speciality** or studio-like deliverable automatically. A film edited by someone may also be shot by them, but that is a per-project credit.

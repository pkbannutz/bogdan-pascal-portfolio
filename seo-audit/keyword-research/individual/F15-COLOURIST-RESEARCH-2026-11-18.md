# F15 / 30: Colourist

**Individual audit:** 18 November 2026. **Market:** UK English film, commercial, music-video, branded content, documentary and post-production crew hire. **Source category:** F15 Colourist (CORE).  
**Verdict:** **CORE verified recognizable UK professional job title**, with an explicitly colourist-identified current website and substantial project grading claims. It is the **human professional role delivering F14 Colour Grading Service**, not an unrelated second occupation or separate duplicate page.  
**Best public role:** **Film & Commercial Colourist**. Supporting hiring vocabulary **Freelance Colourist** and **DaVinci Resolve Colourist** if tool/availability actually supported.  
**Research complete, no production website changes.**

## 0. Commercial identity and architecture decision

**F15 person = the colourist**, who shapes and matches the image of a film under creative intent and technical viewing/delivery constraints. **F14 outcome = colour grading**, the service that a director/producer is purchasing. Searchers may type either **`hire a colourist`** or **`colour grading services`** and expect the same skilled person and final images.

Keep a **single primary service URL**: **/filmmaker/services/colour-grading/**. The page already has a visible **FILMMAKER / COLOURIST** role and unique frame explorer with six film examples. It is stronger than launching a thin `/colourist/` copy merely to catch person queries. If future editorial case studies need unique URLs, distinguish individual projects/technique, not the identical service.

**F15 differentiator:** a working DoP who personally grades can understand light, exposure, camera look and final storytelling end to end. This can be a credible advantage for brands and other DoPs without implying that every graded project is also personally shot, or that the owner has a calibrated Grade 1 HDR mastering suite.

## 1. Formal role boundaries and hiring context

[ScreenSkills Colourist (Film and TV)](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/post-production/colourist/) recognises Colourist as its own post craft: balancing and selecting the final colour palette while collaborating with director and DoP. [ScreenSkills Colourist skills](https://www.screenskills.com/skills-checklists/unscripted-tv/post-production-department/colourist-skills/) covers technical colour fidelity and creative decisions. Professional post facilities may employ grading assistant, online editor and colourist as separate grades.

| Producer/buyer | Job to be done | Appropriate F15 individual job | Unsupported related claims |
|---|---|---|---|
| DoP/director | Find a collaborative image/skin-tone/look partner | Freelance Film Colourist; collaborate from look refs to approved grade | User automatically directed or shot the production |
| Commercial/advertising agency | Build polished and consistent brand image | Commercial Colourist with available true credited ads | Guaranteed brand sales/conversion or colour accuracy without project calibration |
| Music video producer | Grade stylised performance / music promo | Music Video Colourist, if role personally confirmed | VFX artist, show DIT, or music-video director as automatic extra job |
| Remote producer/post supervisor | Deliver grade from locked cut and supplied media | Remote grading workflow only if owner has monitoring and client-review practice | Full remote Grade 1 calibrated attended facility, certified HDR services without evidence |
| Studio/online facility | Hire a colourist for a defined session | Day-rate/session-based colourist work if available and suitable | Online conform editor, mastering engineer and grading assistant included regardless of scale |

**`Colour Grader`** exists colloquially, but less professional film-credit clear than **Colourist**. **`Film Colourist`** (UK spelling) is least ambiguous about the domain, while **`colorist`** (US spelling) can additionally refer to hairstylist, comic book colourists, illustration and software/games. The word's search volume is NOT direct production demand. **Colour correction** is a subtask within much colour-grading, not a separate personal occupation automatically. **Colourist for hire** is a reasonable CTA even where provider volume unknown.

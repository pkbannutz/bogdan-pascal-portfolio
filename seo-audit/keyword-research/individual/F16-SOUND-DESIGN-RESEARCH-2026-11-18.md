## 0. Executive decision

F16 **Sound Design** is a recognised professional audio craft for designing the emotional and physical sound-world around a filmed picture. It can be independently purchased after an edit by directors, producers, brands or a supervising sound editor, often including **sound effects design, ambience, sound editorial, dialogue treatment and appropriate final balancing** within a properly defined brief.

**Do not confuse it with:**
- **F17 Sound Effects / SFX**, to be individually researched next, which is a *subservice/discipline* focused on designing and editing particular effects. F16 is the broader sound experience; a sound effects editor can be a separately credited specialist on larger productions.
- **Production Sound Mixer**, a distinct on-set recording job, even though Bogdan's equipment inventory lists recorders/microphones.
- **Re-recording Mixer or Dubbing Mixer**, specific mix/release stages, multichannel deliverables and additional facility/experience requirements not automatically proved by this service page.
- **Foley Artist** as an independent stage-recording performance profession; layering movement effects in an edit is not proof of a Foley stage, Foley walker credit or fully original synced recording.
- **Composer / Soundtrack Producer**: music composition/score licensing is different from designing sound around the picture. Phrases containing `soundtrack design` may incorrectly suggest composing music.
- **SFX in VFX context**: `SFX` can mean *sound effects*, *special effects* practical on set, or *visual effects* depending on query and buyer; never claim physical pyrotechnics or VFX studio from audio service keywords.

**Site architecture:** keep the existing substantive **/filmmaker/services/sound-design/** as the one F16 sound-service page. Use `SOUND DESIGN & AUDIO POST` and a clearly identified `SOUND EFFECTS` subsection rather than 2 near-duplicate service pages for F16 and F17. Future F17 report will confirm its specific role and keyword strategy. Avoid keyword-stuffed pages with multiple unverified job credits.

## 1. Real buyers, intended audio outcomes and actual responsible roles

| Buyer/brief | What is being purchased | Relevant F16 deliverable | Not automatically included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial/director | Make a finished film feel cinematic and physically present | Carefully built atmosphere, transitions, impacts, whooshes and believable detail | Licensed original musical score and all broadcaster delivery formats |
| Agency/product brand | Sonic interaction for product/action filmed campaign | Product texture, rhythmic sonic movement and ambient atmosphere | Trademark sonic logo, music rights and full audio branding strategy unless separately commissioned |
| Documentary filmmaker | Voice and atmospheres support emotion/clarity | Dialogue cleanup/editing, room tone/ambiences, truthful scene sound and appropriate balance | Manipulative reconstructed factual audio, location recording done by user, ADR stage |
| Action/parkour film editor | Make movement feel weighty and credible | Footfalls/landings, swishes, detail layers, narrative timing | On-set stunt supervision or unsafe physical stunt effects |
| Picture editor/post supervisor | Sound designer joins after rough/fine cut | Sound design to picture, guide mix and stems or final audio output where in scope | Industry-specific 5.1/Atmos/7.1.4 theatrical mix certification |
| Studio sound department | Individual sound editing/design specialist within larger team | Assigned dialogue, ambience or effects tasks to approved spec | Supervising Sound Editor, recording mixer, dubbing editor job credit unless actually contracted |

**Why soundtrack is not just "add effects":** sound design can involve original/recorded and library elements, layering, pitch/space, sonic contrast, dynamics, scene atmosphere and selective silence. It requires rights to use any recordings. Final technical mix, dialogue loudness, stereo vs surround and destination delivery should be agreed, not inferred from ability to create effects.

**Industry evidence:** [ScreenSkills Supervising Sound Editor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/post-production/supervising-sound-editor/) recognises sound editorial responsibility and director/editor collaboration; [AMPS](https://amps.net/) recognises professional screen audio crafts. These sources establish industry role boundaries, not Bogdan's personal membership, awards or supervised team.

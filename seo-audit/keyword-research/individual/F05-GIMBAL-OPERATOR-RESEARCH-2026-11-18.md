# F05 / 30: Gimbal Operator

**Individual research completed:** 18 November 2026. **Market:** UK-English professional crew hire, commercial/music-video/narrative/sport movement, with truthful international availability only after logistics confirmed.  
**Decision:** **REAL separately bookable SPECIALIST CAMERA-OPERATING CAPABILITY**, not necessarily a different departmental grade than Camera Operator (F04). **Best primary label: Gimbal Operator**. Best precise page/role line: **Freelance Gimbal Operator | Commercials, Film & Action**.  
**Status:** RESEARCH ONLY. No live website, SEO, publishing, equipment listing or metadata changes.

## 0. The decision and the necessary distinction

The buying task is **repeatable, stabilised moving-camera shots** performed by an operator who understands balancing, route planning, camera and lens payload, subject timing, crew collaboration and physical safety. This can be hired *as a dedicated skill* alongside a DoP, or be part of a sole Camera Operator's service.

**Best current information architecture:** a **clearly labelled `Gimbal Operator` capability within the existing [DoP / camera service page](../../../filmmaker/services/dop/index.html)**, with one operator/movement reel and a single booking path; F04 remains the overall camera-role category, and F06 `DJI Ronin Operator` is a *technology/vendor-specific variant of F05*, **NOT a second totally new occupation**. A dedicated `/filmmaker/services/gimbal-operator/` page would require substantive unique proof, client FAQs, actual distinct hiring activity and a technical kit section. Do **not** create several manufacturer-named doorway pages.

**Primary business differentiator:** Bogdan knows parkour and stunt movement from inside the performance, which can inform **where to stand, speed matching, distance to moving athletes and timing/cut points**. That's an excellent authentic specialization when supported by real camera-operated footage. It does **not** prove any particular stunt camera operation on `Wicked`, which was a performer role, or authority for high-risk rigging/vehicle shots.

## 1. Buyer scenarios and exact service scope

| Film buyer | Real project request | Good F05 delivery | Beyond F05 unless separately booked/qualified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Director/DoP | Controlled follow shot through a set, corridor or street | Operator with tested handheld gimbal, lens/route balancing, blocking and repeatable takes | Overall lighting design, stunt safety, venue permission or larger camera unit leadership |
| Advertising agency / product brand | Dynamic product reveal or continuous moving shot | Smooth arcs, follows, push-ins, low/high angle choreography within operator rig capacity | Guaranteed crane, car mount, underwater/wire work or industrial robotics |
| Dance/music-video producer | Repeatable performer and ensemble tracking | Movement alignment, rehearsals, timing and continuity with DoP, AC and artist | Official choreographer and fight director duties |
| Sports/event producer | Athletic tracking with spatial awareness and crowd route | Preplanned safe follow movement, crowd/route control handled with production, camera operation | Unsupported running at unsafe distance, tracking vehicle permission or traffic control |
| Lean commercial / factual production | One camera owner/operator who can shift handheld and gimbal | A scoped single-person gimbal/FX3 package if truly available; clear equipment and time estimate | Full lighting team, remote focus technician, cinema crane and custom grip by default |

**Practical operation vs selling equipment:** `Gimbal operator` means a person **operating**, not bare kit hire. A client typing `Ronin hire` may want to rent equipment alone; routing them to a person-based service must say **operator with camera stabiliser**. Extra gear/AC/focus puller is optional and not automatically in fee.

**What not to call it:** `Steadicam Operator` is a different mechanical/body-mounted stabilisation system and specialist skill, despite both roles making stable tracking shots. `Ronin 2 Operator`, `Ronin 4D Operator`, `Easyrig operator`, `remote-head technician`, `vehicle rigging` or `gimbal car mount` must never appear as owned capabilities without direct equipment/experience evidence.

## 1. What P04 actually is and who pays for it

A screen stunt performer executes rehearsed specialist action for the camera within their skills, under the agreed stunt team's plans, safety controls and production requirements. This can include highly physical parkour, falls, acrobatics or other skills **only within demonstrated competence and proper oversight**. A performer may also double a particular actor but stunt doubling is a **specific assignment**, not a generic synonym.

| Booking buyer | Actual purpose | P04 evidence they need | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stunt coordinator / second-unit production | Qualified, trainable person to perform precise action on specific day and setup | Credited filmed action, skill breakdown, up-to-date reel, height/build, work availability, training and production-specific paperwork | Coordinator still leads stunt/safety plan; performer doesn't become coordinator |
| Casting / talent agent | Specialized performer matching character's physical role | Casting specs, look/movement style, actor matching if double, credits | No blanket `stunt double` claim without matching contracts |
| Commercial director / production company | Authentic, controlled visual action for branded film | Good movement footage, proof of professional set experience, producer contact | Agency decides classification/insurance and who must supervise |
| Choreography/stunt team | Actor-facing or performance support and physically executed movement | Exact previous responsibilities and performer-cooperation | Fight direction, stunt supervision and choreography are not interchangeable |

**Role differentiation:** P01 Parkour Athlete, P02 Freerunning Performer, P03 Parkour Coordinator and P04 Stunt Performer may be delivered by the same human but **the job booking/contract/credit and safety arrangement can differ**. P05 Stunt Coordinator is a substantially broader role. SPACT or special action extras are **not** synonymous with professional stunt performers: production classification, experience and terms matter. Do not claim "BSR member", "fully registered", "SPACT", "stunt double" or any protection/insurance by default.

## 6. Verified professional industry role and commercial competition

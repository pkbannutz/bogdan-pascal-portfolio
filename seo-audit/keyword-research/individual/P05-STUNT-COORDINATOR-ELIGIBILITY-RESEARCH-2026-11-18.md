## 1. Why P05 is not a synonym for P03

| Dimension | P03 Parkour Coordinator / Consultant | P05 Stunt Coordinator |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Specialist advice/planning for parkour movement, performance, camera, rehearsal, routes and talent | Plans and oversees **stunt action broadly**, department/personnel, rehearsals, safety-critical setup and supervision |
| Buyer | Director, producer or the **existing lead stunt coordinator** needing parkour knowledge | Producer/film production hiring **stunt-department lead** |
| Physical disciplines | Parkour/freerunning specialist expertise | May require responsible coordination of multiple action types with team specialists: fights, falls, wire, vehicles, water, fire, effects etc. as required by production |
| Decision rights | Collaborate, advise on creative movement and realistic limits | Supervisory authority in the agreed stunt role, including safety controls, rehearsal progression, performer suitability and stop-work escalation |
| Risk / paperwork | Contribute specialist knowledge, route/feasibility observations | Work with production H&S, stunt planning/risk assessments, insurer/venue, performer qualifications, medical/emergency arrangements and crew briefings |
| Cast/director relationship | Coach or source specialist athletes **when verified** | Plan and supervise stunt performers, doubles, assistant coordinators, safety-critical personnel within the stunt operation |
| Evidence needed | Actual parkour advisory/choreography/coordination engagements | Exact personally credited **Stunt Coordinator** assignments; verified safety competence and crew leadership; referenceable producers/stunt team |
| Equivalent SEO name? | **NO** | **NO** |

A specialist can occasionally perform part of a wider action team's planning. **That does not itself establish credited leadership of the complete stunt operation.** Similarly, an actor/acrobat/stunt performer with major film work does **not automatically** hold stunt coordinator competence for unrelated hazards. The reverse is also true: a coordinator may be skilled in a particular niche but must collaborate with specialists where needed.

## 6. Industry bodies, regulation and critical accuracy

### A. UK Equity, strongest legal and professional distinction

**[Equity official advice, engaging stunt professionals](https://www.equity.org.uk/advice-and-support/know-your-rights/engaging-stunt-co-ordinators-and-stunt-performers)**, inspected 18 November 2026:
- Producers/engagers should choose on ability, competency and track record.
- **There is no legal requirement to hire members of a particular organization.**
- Stunt Coordinators and Performers can be associated with multiple organizations or neither, and still have accreditations/experience.
- Producers must conduct appropriate due diligence for a safety-critical industry.

**Implication:** It is wrong to say someone needs a particular union/BSR membership by law to legally coordinate any UK stunt. It is equally wrong to use this rule to pretend anyone with parkour experience can take charge of a production stunt department. Specific contracts, insurers, productions and safety duties determine practical acceptability.

### B. Film London, practical regional filming safety expectations

**[Film London guidance](https://filmlondon.org.uk/filming-in-london/make-sure-its-safe)**, inspected 18 Nov 2026:
- Advises **all stunts be under direct supervision of a recognized BSR Action Coordinator or person of similar experience**.
- Equivalent experience is not rhetorical: documented comparable stunt coordination/performance, recent safety training, familiarity with UK H&S rules, recognized abilities and professional practice are among its stated measures.
- Where the coordinator doubts the competence of a performer, they should not be overruled by the cast/crew.
- This is strong **industry/location safety guidance**, not an automatically universally applicable statute, and should be implemented alongside the actual production's risk/insurance process.

### C. British Stunt Register, prominent industry pathway

**[British Stunt Register](https://www.bsr.co/)** has distinct `Coordinator` and `Performer` searches and describes stunt coordinators as people able to break down scripts and provide safe filmed action. As checked 18 November 2026, it showed 119 coordinators and 529 performers (a **current roster snapshot**, not all UK workers or demand).

**[Production Guild BSR partner profile](https://productionguild.com/our-partners/partner-directory/british-stunt-register/)** says BSR has a structured, peer-reviewed progression from Probationary Stunt Performer through Stunt Coordinator. Exact grade pathways/course requirements can change. **Do not present an old 2021 PDF as the definitive 2026 regulation or infer Bogdan's grade.**

**[Other production guide, Take 3](https://www.take3agency.com/stunts)** reflects the practical buyer workflow: a full stunt team/lead coordinator directs specific assignments and reviews performers' proven abilities. It is a commercial agency, not a regulator.

**General conclusion:** In a risky multi-disciplinary department, competency and specified authority are the key gating factors. Stunt work can trigger serious health, safety, insurance and contractual duties. Bogdan should market what he has proven, not use a wider title for larger apparent SEO volume.

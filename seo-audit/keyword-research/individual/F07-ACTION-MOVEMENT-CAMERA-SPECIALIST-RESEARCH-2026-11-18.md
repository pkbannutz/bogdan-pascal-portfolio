# F07 / 30: Action / Movement Camera Specialist

**Individual audit completed:** 18 November 2026. **Primary market:** UK-English film/TV commercials, sport/parkour branded content, music videos and camera departments filming physically complex performers. **Status:** RESEARCH COMPLETE ONLY. **No production website, SEO, assets or metadata edited.**

## 0. Executive judgment

**F07 is a real, commercially differentiated PRODUCTION SPECIALISM**, but **`Action Camera Specialist` is not a universally recognised formal camera-department job grade**. It is best marketed as an honest *specialisation of a professional camera operator/DoP*, not as a separate senior credit or licence.

**Best human-facing name:** **Action & Movement Camera Operator**, optionally **Parkour & Stunt-Aware Camera Operator**.  
**Best supporting searchable description:** **Specialist Camera Work for Parkour, Stunts & Fast-Moving Performers**.  
**When offering own creative cinematography:** **Action Cinematography & Movement-Led Filming**, with footage that Bogdan personally filmed/operated.  
**What NOT to use as main title:** `Action Camera Operator` alone, because it can mean *operating an action camera (GoPro/DJI Action)* rather than operating a camera near physical action. `Stunt Camera Operator` is recognisable among specialist peers but may imply hazardous stunt-unit experience, approvals and credits. Use in accurate context when personally demonstrated.

**Site strategy:** A distinct **specialist reel and discoverable content block** on existing `/filmmaker/services/dop/` and links from relevant case-study projects. This does not automatically justify a fourth new URL parallel to F04 Camera Operator, F05 Gimbal Operator and F06 DJI Ronin Operator. A dedicated future page could become justified **because the buyer task and proof genuinely differ** (action coordination, athlete timing, movement camera language) if there are enough unique sequences/clients, not simply because a term exists in the inventory.

**High-fit business differentiator:** Bogdan is an active parkour/freerunning athlete and professional stunt/acrobat performer *and* a working commercial DoP/camera operator. He understands movement from the athlete's perspective and framing from the cinematographer's perspective. That is a much more defensible unique selling point than claiming to be "the best stunt camera operator" or claiming all major films he performed in were filmed by him.

## 1. Actual buyer and the measurable service promise

| Buyer | Their specific problem | F07 work product | Boundary / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial creative / sportswear agency | Film quick, physically demanding parkour, freerunning, dance or athletic motion without losing the moment | Accurate shot blocking, follow distance and camera position planned around action, rehearsed camera rhythm | Does not imply the operator designed the athlete's stunt, controls all casting or can guarantee every trick |
| DoP / action second unit | A camera operator who can read timing, trajectories and changing elevation | Skilled camera movement integrated with action, composition, cut points and repeatable coverage | Production-appointed Stunt Coordinator or safety lead supervises stunt risk, not F07 camera specialist by default |
| Parkour and freerunning athlete/brand | Capture sport with authenticity instead of generic shaky follow footage | Knowing performer line, take-off/landing path, wide/close/POV alternatives and lens perspective | No promise of unsafe rooftop pursuit or untested camera rig |
| Music video/dance director | Fast, synchronized movement and subject-to-camera energy | Tracking passage, over/under performance angles, controlled gimbal/handheld style | Not automatically official choreography/movement director role |
| FPV/drone production | Ground-and-air movement coverage on a complex action brief | Ground camera part and coordination with actual FPV pilot if booked separately | F08 FPV flight legality/safety, certificates and equipment require their separate audit; no assumption F07 implies drone authorization |
| Stunt action production | Dynamic action coverage and precise coordination with rehearsal | Camera follow sequence as contracted under DoP/2nd unit/stunt team | Not blanket permission for dangerous close proximity, vehicle rigs, wirework, parkour rooftop chase, stunt supervision or rigging |

**True technique is not just "run fast with a gimbal."** A competent action camera specialist must coordinate **camera operator physical route, performer line and stopping zones, clearances, focus and lens/field-of-view, levels, lighting changes, rehearsed speeds, frame transitions, line-of-sight, cuttable coverage, crew spotters and fatigue** with the real production team. Some moves are better static, long-lens or remote, rather than close chase. Marketing must foreground practical repeatability and controlled creativity, not dangerous stunts.

**F07 scope versus other audited categories:**
- **P01 / P02**: Bogdan being filmed as a parkour/freerunning performer, **in front** of the camera.
- **P03 / P08**: parkour coordination or specific movement choreography; camera collaboration is not automatic camerawork credit.
- **P04**: stunt performer, not stunt camera operator.
- **F02/F03**: overall image-making DoP/Cinematographer.
- **F04**: general Camera Operator, a real film crew job.
- **F05**: Gimbal Operator, technique/platform: F07 can use gimbal, handheld, tripod, fixed or other suitable camera.
- **F06**: DJI Ronin, specific device and currently inconsistent inventory, **not definition of F07**.
- **F08**: FPV Drone Filming, a separately regulated and technical airborne service, still awaiting independent analysis.

A truthful personal site should prioritise the first, describe actual collaborative support where arranged, and avoid selling a company/department or devices not provided.

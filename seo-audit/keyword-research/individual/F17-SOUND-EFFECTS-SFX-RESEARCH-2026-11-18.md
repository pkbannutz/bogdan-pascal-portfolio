# F17 / 30: Sound Effects / SFX

**Individual research:** 18 November 2026. **Market:** UK English, film, commercials, branded video and screen post-production. **Research status:** DONE, with personal sound-effects credits and exact F17 long-tail demand still to verify. **Public website:** NOT MODIFIED.

## 0. Executive decision

**Sound Effects Editing** is a genuine professional film-audio specialty, but for this portfolio it belongs as a **distinct bookable capability inside the existing Sound Design service**, not an automatically separate landing page. The person title is **Sound Effects Editor** when supported by an actual individual production role; the deliverable is **Sound Effects Editing & Design for Film and Commercials**. Use `SFX` only alongside the full words `sound effects`, since in production it can also mean physical/special effects. The existing sound-design page offers action detail, atmosphere and an interactive sound-layer demonstration, but its displayed films do not independently prove Bogdan held the named effects-editor credit on each project.

**Classification:** CORE craft / F16 subservice; a different on-large-productions specialist occupation, not a new independent offer page by default. **Confidence:** high for role naming and industry use; medium for present personally documented project proof; low for unmeasured exact buyer long tails and conversion rates.

## 1. The actual job, buyers and boundaries

| UK production buyer | Why they hire an effects specialist | Bounded service to describe | Must not assume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial director, agency or editor | Precisely timed impacts, whooshes, transitions, tactile product sounds, movement accents | **Sound effects editing and designed SFX to picture** | Licensed bespoke music, complete audio identity campaign, final broadcast mix |
| Narrative film sound team | Diegetic object movement, environmental action, synchronized cue editing | **Sound Effects Editor** supporting the sound supervisor or mixer | Supervising Sound Editor job, cinema re-recording mix |
| Documentary editor | Subtle location/room detail and supporting atmosphere that respects facts | **Effects and ambience editorial** with provenance discipline | Fabricating a documentary event or implying effects are original sync production sound |
| Parkour/action film producer | Footfalls, landings, fabric, motion, spatial action and edits synchronized to frame | **Action sound-effects design** if personal authored clips verified | Stunt coordination, practical explosions or pyrotechnics |
| Foley team | Receives or edits newly recorded footstep/cloth/prop tracks | **Foley editing** only when actually done | Being a **Foley Artist**, Foley walker, Foley stage owner or Foley mixer |

**Role separation verified:** [ScreenSkills Sound Effects Editor skills](https://www.screenskills.com/skills-checklists/unscripted-tv/post-production-department/sound-effects-editor-skills/) identifies the specific effects editorial craft; [ScreenSkills Sound Editor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/post-production/sound-studios/sound-editor-post-production/) includes library, location and Foley elements; [ScreenSkills Foley Artist](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/post-production/sound-studios/foley-artist-post-production/) identifies physically performed/recorded synchronous effects as another profession. [AMPS](https://amps.net/about) recognises Sound Designer and Sound Editor as distinct professional sound categories. This does not establish personal membership or film credits.

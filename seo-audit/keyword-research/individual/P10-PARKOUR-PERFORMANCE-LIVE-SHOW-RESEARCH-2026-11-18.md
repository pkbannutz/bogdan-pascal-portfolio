# P10 / 30: Parkour Performance / Live Show

**Individual research:** 18 November 2026. **Primary search market:** UK English; booking can be international subject to actual logistics. **Status:** COMPLETED RESEARCH, NOT IMPLEMENTED. **No website changes.**

## 0. Executive verdict

**Category:** CORE/ADJACENT, with **strong documented live performance history** and a **distinct event-buyer job** from P01 on-camera parkour, P02 freerunning specialist, P06 acrobat and P09 action sports brand casting.

**Best current commercial offer:** **Parkour & Freerunning Live Performer** (personal bookable talent). **Best event-buyer title:** **Parkour & Freerunning Live Performance**. A full **Parkour Live Show / Team** is an *additional production package*, **not automatically verified as an available turnkey service** merely because Bogdan once performed with OK WorldWide or other teams. Do not claim to provide a roster, structures, staging, rigging, pyrotechnics or event-wide risk ownership until personally confirmed.

**Content strategy:** Show live performance as a distinct **booking use case and evidence collection within /athlete/**. For larger event enquiries, route buyers to a scoped intake. A separate URL is only justified once there is unique current live performance footage, actual show-package offer, technical/logistical information and a different audience task. Do not publish a "parkour team for hire" page based on prior ensemble credits alone.

## 1. Who buys, and how this differs from other 30 categories

| Buyer | Job-to-be-done | Correct P10 offer | Wrong implied offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event producer / festival | In-person athletic spectacle under stage/venue conditions | Solo parkour or acrobatic performer, or confirmed ensemble collaboration | Guaranteed full team supplied, venue-approved obstacle stage |
| Sports brand / activation agency | Timed action demonstrations or a rehearsed sequence near brand audiences | Live performance and separately agreed rehearsal/brand usage | Annual brand ambassadorship or influencer reach guaranteed |
| Broadcast / live TV producer | Repeatable choreography performed for a live audience or television segment | One castable live-show athlete; P08 choreography only when contracted | Production dance choreographer, full stunt safety coordinator |
| Fashion show / creative producer | Athletic intervention in a runway/show visual | Parkour/freerunning movement performer | Fashion show producer or runway creative director |
| Corporate conference / launch | Short live movement feature, possibly alongside another act | Defined solo appearance and planned performance | Invented stage equipment, insurance certification or specific show duration |

**Distinct real customer expectation:** where P01 can be hired for an on-camera shoot, P10 requires **event logistics**: audience/venue separation, practical structure, safe rehearsal, timetable, sound/music rights, surface/landing plan, venue approvals and event producer/appointed safety responsibilities. These are scope-planning questions, not blanket promises that Bogdan manages every safety approval.

**Term boundaries:** `parkour show` tends to signify an act/event package; `parkour performance` can mean a single athlete; `freerunning live performer` clarifies artist style; `stunt show` can imply fire, vehicles, explosions and broad stunt work beyond P10. Do not equate them.

# F13 / 30: Film / Narrative Editing

**Individual research:** 18 November 2026. **Market:** UK-English narrative film, documentary, short films and director-led picture editing. **Scope:** CORE film-editing craft, with stronger individual narrative film editing credits **STILL TO VERIFY**. **No website changes.**

## 0. Decision

**Best recognised professional title: Film Editor**. Best service category: **Film & Documentary Editing**. **Narrative Film Editor** and **Story-Led Picture Editing** are supporting descriptions, not proof of edited feature-film credits.

F13 represents a **genuine distinct client and editorial brief** from F12 Commercial Video Editing, even though both involve the same professional skill and can reasonably share the established **/filmmaker/services/editing/** page. Fiction and documentary producers need performance, structure, dialogue, continuity and director collaboration. Commercial producers frequently request brand-message cuts, campaign versions and short formats. **Do not create a duplicate narrative-editor landing page** without unique personally edited film credits and evidence.

**Honest portfolio gate:** The current project audit establishes many Bogdan **DoP** credits and some Director+DoP or Co-DoP, but does not conclusively establish every displayed film was **personally edited** by him. Being a professional filmmaker and editor is credible; a featured film on an editing page is not automatically an individual Edit credit. Don't imply he personally edited **Wicked, Cinderella or Jingle Jangle**, where his documented role is performance/stunts.

## 1. Role scope and actual buyer needs

[ScreenSkills film and TV Editor](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/post-production/editor-film-and-tv-drama) describes assembling captured scenes and shaping a story through footage, performance, rhythm and director notes. [ScreenSkills Editor Skills](https://www.screenskills.com/skills-checklists/scripted-film-and-tv/editorial-department/editor-skills/) covers editorial collaboration, coverage, structure, sound and post workflow.

| Buyer | Actual job | Specific evidence wanted | Not included automatically |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiction short-film director | Scene/take choices, pace, performance, tension, fine cut | Named personal Editor credit, short film, collaborators | Cinematography, director, full film ownership |
| Documentary director | Interviews, actuality, archive structure, ethical/story clarity | Credited personally edited documentary | Research clearance, archive licensing, fact-checking by default |
| Indie feature producer | Longer edit, assemblies, feedback, turnovers, picture lock | Feature editor credit and rigorous file organisation | Not currently confirmed from repo project record |
| Brand storytelling producer | Longer branded narrative rather than pure promotion | Specific editor role on a branded story | Automatically all social versions, grade and music licences |
| Music-video director | Performance/music pacing | Personally credited music-video Editor | DoP or performer credit is not editing proof |

**Professional distinctions:** Film Editor / Picture Editor is a meaningful screen role; **Offline Editor**, **Online Editor**, **Assistant Editor**, **Finishing Editor**, **Post Supervisor**, **Director**, **Script Editor** are different responsibilities depending on production. Do not use them as interchangeable SEO keywords. Broad "picture editor" Google interest also includes photography apps.

**Movement advantage:** Bogdan's athletic/stunt experience can inform judging the right action frame and timing in a film **he actually edits**, but it is not a camera/editing credit for major films on which he was performing.

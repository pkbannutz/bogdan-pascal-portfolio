## 1. Professional role, buying task and boundaries

[ScreenSkills' Camera Operator Skills](https://www.screenskills.com/skills-checklists/scripted-film-and-tv/camera-department/camera-operator-skills/) says the scripted film/HETV operator **reports to the DoP**, helps plan movement/framing/composition, operates equipment, checks image quality and coordinates with director, 1st AC/focus puller and grip. Responsibility varies by production size. [National Careers Service](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/tv-or-film-camera-operator) separately recognizes the occupation.

| Real buyer | What is being hired | Role-specific deliverables | Not automatically included |
|---|---|---|---|
| DoP/camera department | Operator executes the camera movement and composition set by the DoP/director | Framing, lens/camera handling, pan/tilt, handheld, repeatability, communication with AC/grip | **Overall DoP lighting look authority**, line production or film directing |
| Commercial/music-video producer | Skilled camera operating to capture planned moving shots | Camera movement, practical blocking, safe on-set teamwork | Editing, whole production, stunt safety unless separately contracted |
| Small-crew factual/brand production | One person can light and operate subject to project scope | Lighting camera operator/owner-operator where agreed | Being every lighting/rigging department, camera assistant and sound team at once |
| Specialist action production | Operating near a performer, camera movement choreographed with action | Timing, rehearsed proximity, awareness of subject/route and capture needs | Directing dangerous action, **Stunt Coordinator** or licensed vehicle rigging |
| Larger crew camera unit | Second/B camera operation under DoP | On-set shot coverage, movement, matching the look | Automatically a 1st AC, focus puller, DIT, grip or aerial pilot |

**Key F04-versus-F02 distinction:** the same person **can** work as a DoP on Project A and as an operator under another DoP on Project B. The roles are contractually/organisationally different. The main talent-site navigation should let clients know **both hire paths** without pretending separate personal entities or requiring 2 near-duplicate portfolios.

**F04 versus F05:** `Gimbal Operator` is an **operating technique/platform specialization**, not universally a separate camera-department rank. It may be hired separately on projects with specialised moves and is F05's subject. `Steadicam Operator` is a distinct stabilisation system/technical discipline, NOT a generic label for someone with a DJI handheld motorised gimbal.

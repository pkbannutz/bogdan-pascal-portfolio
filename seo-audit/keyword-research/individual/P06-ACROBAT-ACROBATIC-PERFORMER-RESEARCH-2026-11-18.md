# P06 / 30: Acrobat / Acrobatic Performer

**Date:** 18 November 2026  
**Research status:** DONE, recommendations only. **Production website unchanged.**  
**Market:** UK-English commercial film, castable screen talent, music video and separately live/event buyers. **One original category only:** P06.  
**Verdict:** **Core, independently bookable physical performance speciality**, with substantial first-party evidence; not merely a synonym for P01 parkour or P02 freerunning. **Primary naming: Acrobat & Acrobatic Performer**, usually shortened to **Acrobatic Performer** in specific hiring copy. **Best placement:** clear specialist service within the existing authoritative athlete profile; do not create an automatic near-duplicate URL.

## 1. Decision and positioning

The evidence is strongest for **ground-based acrobatics, tumbling, trampoline-informed movement and on-camera/live physical performance**. Buyers can hire an acrobatic performer without needing a parkour chase or a stunt-classified assignment. Distinct visual deliverables and buyers justify naming P06 directly.

- **Visitor-facing best name:** **Acrobat & Acrobatic Performer**; shorter section header **ACROBATIC PERFORMANCE**.
- **Film/TV option:** **Acrobatic Performer for Film, TV & Commercials**.
- **Event option:** **Acrobat for Live Shows & Brand Events** (P10 overlaps, and event-specific acts must be confirmed).
- **Avoid unverified scope:** aerial silks/hoop/flying rigs, partner hand-to-hand lifts, Chinese pole, contortion, circus rigging, trapeze, full gymnastics disciplines, certified coaching and performer flying supervision. Competitor agencies list such disciplines, but personal credits do not substantiate them.
- **Hiring proof to surface:** actual Bogdan-acrobat footage and exact role, including BRIT Awards/Greatest Showman performance, Expo/Dubai advertising performance, music video acrobatics, stage and TV results.

These are first-party descriptions, **not universal proof that every named client's media and every choreographed routine may be reused or that Bogdan acted as the production's credited acrobat soloist**.

## 6. Trends and seasonality across the four source windows

Archived raw Google Trends: [5-year and 12-month panels](../athlete-data-raw-2026-11-18.json) and [2004-present/3-month](../athlete-discovery-and-long-trends-2026-11-18.json). Data are normalized **within** comparison groups, cannot compare values across windows as absolute volume.

- **2004 to Nov 2026:** `acrobatics` averaged **1** in the broad five-term normalized panel (parkour, freerunning, stunts, acrobatics, extreme sports). This is **not** human acrobat booking history.
- **Five years:** `acrobatics` averaged **10** in the broad five-term panel; a separate 5-year panel dominated by the word `acrobat` yielded **acrobat 68** and all niche performer phrases near zero, indicating a likely *wrong comparison base* because Adobe.
- **12 months:** the `acrobat` comparison dominated at **83** relative points, while specific performer phrases showed zeros under normalization. Do NOT conclude `acrobatic performer` is unpopular or demand has vanished.
- **Recent three months:** `acrobatics` averaged **4** relative points in its broad panel. Small sample and panel differences forbid declaring an annual seasonality trend.
- **Provider monthly history:** `acrobatic performer` 10-40 rounded searches, `acrobat for hire` 0-10, `professional acrobat` generally 1,300-1,900, but no booking trend inferred.

**Verdict on trend:** enduring subject awareness, **no reliable time series for qualified commercial acrobat hiring**. Do not create seasonal offer pages based on unconnected cultural or software search spikes.

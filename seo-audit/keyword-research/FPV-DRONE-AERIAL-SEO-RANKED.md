## Executive decision

**Recommended primary visitor-facing offering:** **FPV Drone Filming & Aerial Cinematography**.

**Recommended individual booking-role labels:** **FPV Drone Pilot** (rapid, close-action, immersive camera routes) and **Drone Operator / Aerial Cinematographer** (stabilised cinematic coverage). A solo pilot and a fully staffed aerial unit are **not the same deliverable**. Mention camera operator/crew only when actually provided.

**Best concise proposed hero service line (VISIBLE, not applied):**  
`FPV DRONE PILOT · AERIAL CINEMATOGRAPHER`

**Proposed accessible first paragraph:**  
`FPV drone filming for action, tracking and immersive fly-throughs, alongside stabilised aerial cinematography for films, commercials and branded productions. I plan each camera move around the story, the location and the production brief.`

**Proposed search title:**  
`FPV Drone Filming & Aerial Cinematography | Bogdan Pascal`

**Why this naming beats guessing from raw volume:** Specific film-client phrases fit professional operator hire and distinct shooting techniques. Those smaller labels can help a production hire the right operator. A huge consumer FPV drone trend does **not** automatically indicate increased commissioning of film crews.

## 6. UK drone legality and credentials: service trust without misleading labels

**EVIDENCED from the [CAA's current “Flying drones for work”](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/open-category/moving-on-to-more-advanced-flying/flying-drones-for-work/) and [CAA A2 CofC guidance](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/specific-category/remote-pilot-qualifications/a2-certificate-of-competency-a2-cofc/), checked 18 Nov 2026:**
- The UK system distinguishes **Open A1/A2/A3** from **Specific Category** operations. Requirements depend on the drone and **risk of the flight**, not a universal old-style commercial flight licence.
- CAA explicitly states the old **PfCO was withdrawn at end of 2020** and has no direct replacement. Avoid `PfCO licensed` or `fully CAA licensed` as a casual SEO slogan.
- Organisations operating drones require relevant **Operator ID**, remote-pilot competency, applicable authorisations, and **third-party insurance for non-recreational use**, with details depending on operation.
- A2 Certificate of Competency is relevant to **Open Near People (A2)** as defined under updated 2026 CAA rules. It is not a blanket permission for any flying over crowds, indoor conditions, congested airspace or international jobs.
- Specialist category work requires applicable CAA Operational Authorisation; individual/organisation paperwork and permissions are flight-specific. FPV may require an observer and additional procedures depending on the type of operation.
- **VERIFY before stating on site:** current IDs, A2 CofC validity, insurance, eligible drones and category, permission/airspace procedures, pilot geography, whether relevant EU/EASA permissions are arranged. Never infer from possession of equipment alone.

**Practical CTA trust cue (conditional, if verified):** `Share your location, dates, shot brief and intended subject. I'll assess the platform, permissions and crew required.` This sounds credible without an inaccurate safety certification badge.

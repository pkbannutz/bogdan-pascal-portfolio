## Executive decision

**Lead with “FPV Drone Pilot” and “Aerial Cinematography” as complementary, distinct production capabilities.** Keep one coherent existing service page rather than separate duplicate synonym pages. Human service labels should serve film, advertising and branded-content clients rather than chasing broad real-estate, surveying, hobby or drone training searches.

**Proposed visible descriptor under existing “CAMERA IN MOTION” H1:**

> FPV DRONE PILOT · AERIAL CINEMATOGRAPHY

**Proposed first paragraph:**

> FPV drone filming and cinematic aerial coverage for commercials, film and branded content. I plan and fly precise camera moves, from close-action tracking to wide establishing shots, choosing the right drone and flight plan for the production.

**Two client routes:** `DISCUSS AN FPV SHOT` for close-action fly-through/chase movement, and `DISCUSS AERIAL FILMING` for controlled conventional drone coverage. One shared contact pathway is sufficient. Use the existing clean visual style; do not promise operations, permits or camera systems not confirmed for a real brief.

**Critical finding:** The page's selected film links demonstrate broader cinematography and camera movement, **not independently verified FPV footage for each listed project**. Label clips by actually performed role before claiming FPV client examples. Project attribution is more important for production trust than adding another keyword to the H1.

## 3. UK Google Trends, checked for four time windows

**Raw evidence:** [six UK web-search panels](fpv-trends-raw-2026-11-18.json), downloaded 18 November 2026. Same-panel terms: `fpv drone filming`, `fpv drone pilot`, `drone filming`, `aerial cinematography`, `aerial filming`. Separate five-term specialist comparison was also collected. **Trends 0 to 100 is relative within each panel, not monthly search counts.** Small niche term zeros are sparse/suppressed observations, not evidence that the service has zero buyers.

- **2004 to present:** `drone filming` has a much more observable long-term signal than specific FPV job titles; raw all-time panel averages approx **9** for drone filming, **3** for aerial filming, other specialist terms at or near 0. Drone filming interest appears more detectable in recent years; broad historical panel normalization cannot quantify hires.
- **Five years:** broad five-term comparison average index approx **8** for drone filming; the remaining narrow phrases are too sparse to score reliably. 2026 samples show large variation versus earlier years, but peaks can distort normalization. Do not infer a smooth structural FPV boom from these series.
- **Twelve months:** same five-term comparison `drone filming` average index approx **27**. Other specialist labels often 0 due to scaling. **Independent Google Ads monthly estimates:** drone videographer about 390 down to 260 by October 2026; drone filming had an estimated March 2026 spike of 480 but was 140 by October; FPV drone pilot was mostly 20 to 40 throughout. `fpv drone filming` had an exceptional November 2025 value 170 and otherwise commonly 0 to 30, so its 20 average is unstable for forecasting.
- **Three months:** relative Google Trends mostly shows generic `drone filming` with specialist phrases at sparse zeros. A final-day sample can be incomplete; do not use its high normalized number as acceleration.
- **Seasonality:** Insufficient evidence for repeatable annual *production booking* seasonality from one 12-month Google Ads series and sparse Trends panels. The March 2026 drone-filming spike is one event, not a proven production buying season.

**Interpretation:** Keep stable professional labels rather than rewriting the hero after a one-month spike. FPV is a specialist B2B role with low public search volume; producer relationships, targeted credits, reels and direct referrals may be as important as generic SEO.

### VISIBLE

**Preserve H1:** `CAMERA IN MOTION.`, complemented immediately by actual crawlable text:

```text
FPV DRONE PILOT · AERIAL CINEMATOGRAPHY

FPV drone filming and cinematic aerial coverage for commercials, film and branded content. I plan and fly precise camera moves, from close-action tracking to wide establishing shots, choosing the right drone and flight plan for the production.
```

**Use two buyer-facing capability headings** rather than generic `GET CLOSE` and `GO WIDE` alone (the latter can remain as artistic treatments):
- `FPV DRONE FILMING`: action tracking, fast low passes, camera movement through spaces when technically safe and feasible.
- `AERIAL CINEMATOGRAPHY`: establishing shots, location scale, smooth reveals and controlled conventional drone moves.

**Do not imply all four selected films feature personally operated FPV shots** unless verified, and avoid implying specific camera sensor, heavy-lift rig, indoor permissions or commercial authorisations without documentation. Provide one verified short **FPV-only reel**, one **aerial-only** clip or correctly-labelled shot list; these could outperform more text in persuading a producer.

**CTA:** `DISCUSS YOUR FPV SHOT` or `ENQUIRE ABOUT AERIAL FILMING` with brief instructions to send location/country, dates, action, shot references, environment and usage/delivery needs. A normal contact form or email is sufficient; this is not a booking engine.

### BACKEND / compliance / performance

1. **P0:** Fix canonical, sitemap and deployment whitelist at later authorized implementation, ensuring sitemap includes this service page. Existing audit reports root `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` were 404; fresh remote confirmation was not available in this pass.
2. **P0:** Avoid bogus regulatory claims. CAA's current (2026) guidance distinguishes the A2 CofC for Open category from **Specific category operational authorisation**, and 2026 rules evolved. Check drone ID, aircraft class, weight, airspace, flight category, A2 certificate validity, any operational authorisation, insurance, flight crew/observer requirements, landowner and location permissions for every proposed production. `A2 CofC` does **not** on its own grant universal access to restricted or higher-risk filming. In other countries validate their rules separately.
3. **P1:** Use truthful `Person` and/or `Service` JSON-LD that matches visible offerings and exact credentials. Do not fabricate qualifications; schema is contextual markup, not a ranking shortcut.
4. **P1:** Mobile and browser performance: check actual flight-lab CPU/fps, tab hidden behavior, motion-reduction preferences, touch interactions, screen-reader labels, lazy media and Core Web Vitals on real mobile devices. Do not derive performance scores from image bytes alone.
5. **P1:** Create correctly attributed project proof: operation performed, aircraft/platform if accurately known, scene type, production context and link to one of 23 real work pages where demonstrably shot by Bogdan. No competitor-style case studies invented.
6. **P1:** Use Google Search Console for real indexing, UK query impressions/clicks/position by page, branded vs nonbranded, and conversion logging of qualified FPV versus aerial enquiries. No actual GSC access or bookings measured.
7. **P2:** Only consider a separate dedicated FPV URL **after** there is materially distinct FPV footage, client case-study copy and clear independently validated search intent. The current single `/fpv/` route is sufficient for initial positioning.

# Sound design, sound effects and film audio post: ranked UK professional naming and SEO

**Status: DONE, RESEARCH ONLY, NOT APPLIED.** **Date:** 18 November 2026. **Existing target:** https://bogdanpascal.com/filmmaker/services/sound-design/. **Market:** UK English Google Search, 64 queries; Google Ads historical estimates Nov 2025–Oct 2026; organic SEO difficulty and machine search-intent classification; UK Google Trends 2004-to-present, 5 years, 12 months, 3 months; seven UK desktop SERP query attempts. **No website source or public search settings edited.**

## Executive professional-naming recommendation

**Primary personal job title:** **Sound Designer (Film & Commercials)**.

**Primary service:** **Film Sound Design & Audio Post-Production**, with subordinate **Sound Effects Editing**, **Dialogue Editing & Cleanup**, **Atmosphere / Ambience Design**, and **Sound Mixing** only to the extent truly offered and evidenced.

**Proposed descriptor immediately next to the artistic `SOUND IS THE FEELING.` H1:**

> FILM SOUND DESIGN · SOUND EFFECTS & AUDIO POST

> Sound design for commercials, branded films and screen stories, from detailed effects and atmosphere to clear dialogue and a balanced final soundtrack.

**Important decision:** Remove **“SFX” as the sole meaning-bearing service title**. “SFX” may mean special/visual effects or sound effects. Explicit `Sound Effects` and `Audio Post` target actual clients with far less ambiguity. SFX remains a useful shorthand for specialists, clearly labeled within an audible-effects context.

**No need for 8 duplicate URLs.** One credible sound-design portfolio page with before/after audio evidence, clear personal sound credits and booking CTA is more useful than separate thin pages for Foley, sound effects, film sound, and dialogue correction synonyms.

## 1. Current site, existing proof, honest role distinction

Inspected `filmmaker/services/editing/index.html`, connected GitHub source, and live HTML separately on 18 November 2026.

- Existing title `Film Editing | Bogdan Pascal`; poetic H1 `THE ART OF THE CUT.`. The hero label says `EDITOR / FILMMAKER`; the body explains narrative structure, rhythm, finishing and delivery. **Existing page has around 1,008 meaningful visible words**, not a thin SEO text page.
- The page already contains a viewing room of real video selections, project navigation, before/after framing exercises, final delivery details, moving-image examples, a RawPot Health **actual landscape film and portrait version**, and a clear enquiry email button with editing-specific subject.
- Important usability safeguard: an interactive **format lab** demonstrates composition choices; the lab is not proof of a specific client export, whereas the real RawPot master and vertical cut **are** concrete platform version examples.
- Videos and multiple preview components are technically heavy; the earlier measured at-scroll image payload on this service was around **7.0 MiB excluding video streams**. Client experience and click-to-play matter as much as another H2 with keywords. Verify actual on-demand video loading, mobile playback, keyboard behavior and first-load LCP before changing.
- Source project-credits audit [23 films](../PROJECT-SEO-ALL-23.md) often credits **Bogdan as DoP** while **BTHEVISUAL** receives post-production credit. **Those are not automatically personal editing credits**. Individual editing of a named project must be verified before marketing `I edited these ads` or generating per-person `editor` JSON-LD.
- The clearly documented **RawPot widescreen/portrait variants** establish actual output examples displayed on page, but **not automatically who performed each exact edit**. Confirm personal edit responsibilities when labeling work.
- User has described editing in DaVinci Resolve; do not make `DaVinci Resolve editor` the headline as it attracts tutorial and software users and makes the public service title software-dependent.
- Pricing and revision rounds should be discussed by scope; never assert fixed numbers of cuts, caption delivery or platform compliance unless those are actual offerings. Keep one existing contact route.

**Service/hiring boundaries:** a freelance video editor is a person booked for picture storytelling and post, a film editor works on film/narrative/longer content, an **online editor** can mean a specialist conform/finishing role, a **colourist** does grade work, a **sound designer/mixer** works on sound. One individual may do several but do not silently imply professional facility/mastering/broadcast compliance without proof.

## 4. Google Trends, four horizons

**Saved source:** [GB normalized five-term panels](editing-data-raw-2026-11-18.json), same keywords per window: `video editor`, `video editing services`, `film editing`, `commercial video editing`, `freelance video editor`. Google Trends values are **relative within each separately normalized panel**; do not compare scores arithmetically between horizons. Small exact commercial terms are often sampled at 0, not literally absent.

| Period | Observed same-panel source | Booking inference |
|---|---|---|
| **2004–Nov 2026** | `video editor` panel relative annual mean ~**27 (2004)**, **49 (2021)**, **61 (2025)**, **80 (2026 YTD)**; `film editing` ~**11 (2004)** and **11 (2026 YTD)**. | Consumer/platform/video-tool interest is large, increasingly so; **not proof freelance editor bookings have similarly grown** |
| **5 years** | Weekly `video editor` annual panel means ~**54 (2022)**, **51 (2024)**, **55 (2025)** and **68 (2026 YTD)**; `film editing` ~**4–9**. | Durable broad interest with some recent relative rise. Specific role search suppressed by broad term. |
| **12 months** | `video editor` ~**68** in sampled 2026 weekly panel; `film editing` ~**9**; `video editing services` ~**1**; freelance/commercial specialty too low to interpret as slope. | Too noisy to infer actual commissioning or best months to seek freelance jobs |
| **3 months** | Daily `video editor` averages ~**38** (this *separate normalized panel*), `film editing` ~**1**, others ~0. | **Do not** interpret 38 vs 68 as an interest collapse. Relative normalization and sampling differ. |

**Trends conclusion:** freelance editor hire is a persistent industry service independent of consumer software spikes. Narrow professional phrases are better judged by real UK hiring competitor services and paid term estimates, not a forced “Google Trends breakout” claim. Google Ads monthly history is in the CSV but no proven recurring client hiring seasonality is established.

## 6. Recommended exact VISIBLE, SEARCH, BACKEND plan [NOT APPLIED]

### VISIBLE: preserve excellent interactive storytelling, but state the job clearly

- **P0 first screen:** Keep `THE ART OF THE CUT.` H1 and filmic aesthetic. Show `FREELANCE COMMERCIAL VIDEO EDITOR` and `Film Editing, Brand Films & Delivery` as immediately readable HTML copy. Do not make producers scroll past interactive experiments to discover the actual hireable role.
- **P0 service outcomes:** two short grouped commitments: **`Edit the story`** (selects, assembly, narrative and pacing) and **`Finish the delivery`** (approved master, adapted format cuts, sound/colour coordination). This is not the same as promising all other departments are automatically included in the editing fee.
- **P0 evidence:** the actual RawPot widescreen and portrait examples already help. Label what was *actually done* and by whom. Do not label all eight selected films as personally edited merely because they appear in the viewing room or have personal DoP credit.
- **P1 CTA:** retain `TELL ME ABOUT THE PROJECT` with editing-specific subject. Request brief, timeline, amount of raw footage, reference, platforms, output ratios, captions, number of review rounds and location of media files as practical input, not a huge form.
- **P1 mobile:** interactive theatre, video preview hover, crop lab and animated video library must be accessible on touch and keyboard, with reasonable data use. Use film posters and click-to-play rather than loading ten videos at first scroll.
- **P2 production style:** software/format specs (DaVinci Resolve, proxies, export codecs) belong in a small workflow/detail area, not title; true deliverables and trust first.

### SEARCH

**Title:** `Commercial Video Editor & Film Editing | Bogdan Pascal`  
**Meta:** `Freelance commercial video editor for brand films, documentaries and campaign content. Story-led editing, social cutdowns and widescreen-to-vertical delivery.`

Alternative if user prioritizes buying service over job title: `Commercial Video Editing & Post-Production | Bogdan Pascal`. Choose based on observed personal vs outsourcing enquiries once indexable and measured, not purely model search volume.

### BACKEND

1. **P0** resolve live `noindex,nofollow` after express approval and confirmation page should be public/indexable; check canonical, header `X-Robots-Tag`, sitemap/robots route and GSC URL Inspection. No live code touched.
2. **P0** adequate contextual role-sensitive project descriptions and no false `editor` claims from company post or personal DoP. `Person`/`Service` schema only for real visible work.
3. **P1** self-canonical `https://bogdanpascal.com/filmmaker/services/editing/`, OG preview consistent with film poster, good descriptive meta text; no `meta keywords` stuffing.
4. **P1** ensure video lazy loading and range requests/thumbnail strategy; first edit theatre may preload metadata only, many project hover-preview videos exist. Measure mobile LCP/INP/CLS and bandwidth before claiming a speed target.
5. **P1** textual/keyboard alternative to format lab and viewer, legible captions, tab focus, valid poster alt and meaningful headings, performance-aware media formats.
6. **P1** link actual verified personal editing project credits back to the editing page, and relevant master-delivery case studies; do not link all DoP films to editing merely because editing was performed by someone on the production.
7. **P1** track Search Console role-qualified search terms and actual **editing enquiries booked**, grouped by commissioned film vs edit-only and post package. No baseline currently available.
8. **P3** new pages for freelance editor, video editing, music video edit, reels and vertical deliverables are **not warranted now**. They are overlaps/supporting subservices, not independently proven distinct landing-page demand.

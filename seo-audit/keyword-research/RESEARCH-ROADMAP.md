## The rule for future AI

**The user has a clear outcome, but their proposed method may be wrong.** Optimize for qualified hiring, truthful professional positioning, audience experience, demonstrated results and effective current SEO techniques. Challenge unsupported assumptions with alternatives. The user wants the **best industry-standard service names**, not just obscure synonyms with traffic numbers. Keyword research informs the names and the supporting discoverable content, but does not dictate meaningless over-optimized copy.

## What counts as a completed service family

A service family can be marked **DONE** only after the report provides:
1. Direct verification of the relevant current website page, role/credit proof and legitimate offered services, distinguishing unsupported or adjacent roles.
2. Research-backed ranking of best **human-facing professional job/service names**, with industry-source evidence and reasons for rejecting weaker titles.
3. Prioritized *client-intent* query clusters, including monthly estimated demand, organic difficulty, confidence/null caveats and relevant geography.
4. Relative Google Trends comparison since 2004 where useful, rolling five years, 12 months and three months, with durable vs seasonal vs anomalous changes distinguished. Never equate Trends indices with absolute monthly searches.
5. Actual relevant Google hiring search result checks, with date, region, device, original rank/source and classification of agencies, portfolios, directories, jobs, editorial pages and sponsored items. No false claims of verified top-10 when incomplete.
6. Clear **VISIBLE** recommended service names/introduction, service proof, page/section structure and appropriate hiring CTA, plus **SEARCH** title/meta and **BACKEND** headings, canonical/schema, asset metadata/internal linking as relevant.
7. Prioritization based on industry recognition, truthful service fit, qualified booking intent and achievable traffic opportunity, not highest volume alone.
8. Precise source citations/paths and acknowledgement of uncertainty, limitations and unresolved research.
9. The finished report committed in this GitHub folder; progression updated here. No public website edits without the user's express implementation request.

## Research workflow, repeated per family

Inspect existing page and industry roles -> classify buyer personas -> establish genuinely recognized service names -> map distinct hiring intents and synonyms -> get UK estimated monthly volumes/organic difficulty/search intent -> study Trends historical/five-year/year/quarter -> confirm what actually ranks and why -> evaluate source quality and real role fit -> recommend visible service names and search/on-page architecture -> document all evidence and completion -> move to next service.

**Important guardrails:**
- Competitor-only services and 61 additional taxonomy categories are *not* automatically offered by Bogdan.
- Existing `MASTER-SERVICE-KEYWORDS.md` covers 91 research categories but most have not had quantitative analysis. Do not claim that every category is done or that the user provides them all.
- Don't turn 5 synonyms into 5 duplicate landing pages. One focused page can cover natural related phrasing.
- Search Console / GA4 connection status not established. Actual query clicks, rankings and conversions remain unknown.
- Do not create fake volume, scores, credentials, or Google rank claims; do not promote paid listings as organic.
- Favor concise chat summaries and complete GitHub documentation, including date/geo/provider and any incomplete tasks.

## Candidate: underwater camera work

Prior candidate taxonomy flags `Underwater cinematography` as **“Scope/availability to confirm”** in [USER-SERVICES-KEYWORDS.md](../USER-SERVICES-KEYWORDS.md) F11. The portfolio and equipment page were reviewed, but no dedicated marketed underwater service page or individually credited underwater production was verified in this pass. The owner may have pool/underwater shooting skill and equipment from personal background, but ability alone is not sufficient evidence to advertise unrestricted `Underwater Cinematographer` or commercial underwater services.

**If a genuine offered skill is confirmed, common UK buyer-facing roles** include `Underwater Camera Operator`, `Underwater Cinematographer`, and `Underwater Filming`. The correct title depends on scope: pool-controlled studio shoots and underwater surface/pool camera operation are not equivalent to open-water scuba cinematography, diving safety supervision, marine wildlife filmmaking or a full dive unit.

**Evidence of actual industry vocabulary, not verified top-ten Google ranking:** [Rob Hill](https://rob-hill.co.uk/), [Rich Stevenson](https://www.richstevenson.co.uk/), [Esprit Film underwater camera](https://www.espritfilm.co.uk/underwater-camera). These pages show operator/filming specializations and dedicated visual proof.

**Gating items before quantitative UK research / client-facing service:**
- Identify personally shot underwater projects and public footage, with exact on-set role and rights to display.
- Specify pool vs open water, depth, camera/housing, lighting/crew, and whether using freediving or scuba.
- Check location-specific diving competency, safety crew, insurance, employer/location regulations, production permission and what is legitimately offered.
- Decide whether this is a genuine recurring paid booking offering. If it is, conduct own GB Ads 12-month demand, organic difficulty, historical/5y/12m/3m Google Trends and film-hire SERP analysis, without assuming generic underwater-photography demand equals booked cinematography.
- If only occasional technique in a DoP package, list accurately as a capability and showcase one real case, **not** create a thin separate service page.

**Other candidate skills:** action/parkour camera work is already included credibly under DoP/Camera/Gimbal; live parkour performance is under Athlete. Other competitors' equipment rental, race footage, performance coaching, motion capture, VFX, whole film production teams or aerial surveying are **not** automatically personal offerings.

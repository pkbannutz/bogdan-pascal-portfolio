## Observed Google Trends patterns and limitations

- Broad `parkour` consistently registers appreciable UK search interest. `stunts`, `acrobatics` and `extreme sports` also register across five years.
- `movement director` registers more reliably than `parkour coordinator` in a niche role-comparison panel, but Google Trends' scale is *relative to the selected terms and period*. It is not comparable to the absolute Google Ads volume table.
- Rare, highly targeted labels such as `parkour performer`, `stunt performer` and `parkour athlete` regularly show 0 when compared alongside the much more popular `acrobat`. That is sampling/normalization suppression, **not** zero actual searches.
- One Google Ads monthly-data series shows an August 2026 spike in searches associated with `freerunning` and its near-identical variants. It needs event/seasonality validation before being called an enduring trend.
- Use three windows: **all-time (since 2004)** for historical context, **5 years** for structural trend/seasonality, **12 months + 3 months** for recent direction. Prefer 5 years for baseline strategy and more recent windows for emerging opportunities, not last-week fluctuations.
- DataForSEO keyword ideas request FAILED (502), so the new phrase discovery phase should be retried only where it improves usefulness.

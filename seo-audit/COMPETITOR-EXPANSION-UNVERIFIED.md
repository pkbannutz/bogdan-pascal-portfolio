# Additional service ideas not verified from sampled competitor URLs

**39 candidate categories × five phrases = 195 exploratory variants.** These are NOT included in the main evidenced total. Reasons include blocked websites, results that were job listings/directories/education pages, weak or ambiguous wording, and terms duplicating existing service intents.

Source: individual search-result URLs from the earlier 300-hit discovery list, including second-pass menu expansion on 37 high-navigation pages. Links are what we observed on those pages. They do NOT establish every service offered on every page of the entire domains, and not every visible link is a service. Only 248 of 274 unique URLs returned HTTP 200. The 26 unsuccessful pages remain unverified. New categories are competitor-side research, NOT assertions that Bogdan currently offers, is qualified for, or should market them. Five search variants are suggested phrases, NOT verified Google query volume.

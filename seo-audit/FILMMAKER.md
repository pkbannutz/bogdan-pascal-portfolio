# Filmmaker SEO: bogdanpascal.com/filmmaker/

**Scope:** Filmmaker, Director of Photography, commercial production, drone/FPV, editing, colour grading, sound design and 23 project case studies.  
**Status:** Audit only, no site changes.

## Confirmed findings

### Landing page: `filmmaker/index.html`
- Title: `Bogdan Pascal | Filmmaker and DoP` is reasonable but not very specific about commercial production.
- Current meta description: `Director of photography and drone operator. Commercials, brand films, music videos and shorts.` It lacks your name and is generic.
- **No H1**. The highly visual hero has `Every frame / on purpose.` in `span` tags; the work cards use H3 elements.
- The page leads with a visually strong portfolio grid and 23 linked films, but has limited crawlable explanation of why a brand or production should hire you.
- `og:image` uses a root-relative path (`/assets/filmmaker/sky-above/t.jpg`) instead of an absolute URL; no self-canonical and no JSON-LD found.
- All 23 grid images have alt attributes and are identified by project. First six thumbnails are marked eager; remaining images lazy. Check above-the-fold image priorities and sizes using Lighthouse rather than assuming poor performance.
- The footer contains email and social links, plus a cross-link to the athlete portfolio.

### Project pages: `filmmaker/work/*/index.html`
- **23 individually named project pages inspected.** Each sampled project page has one H1 and a unique project-specific title.
- **Shared weakness across the 23:** Metadata is mostly generated by `filmmaker/_build/build.py` as `Project name. Category.` instead of a meaningful summary. This pattern is visible across TUI Romania, Yutaka, GeoDomeX, Therme, Speedo, Ffern and other projects.
- Project share images use root-relative `og:image` URLs.
- Sampled stills have `alt` attributes, often mechanical like `GeoDomeX still 1`; there is room for human descriptions on selected meaningful frames.
- No self-canonical and no JSON-LD found in inspected generated pages.
- Project videos use native video elements with `preload="metadata"` and external media sources in the inspected GeoDomeX page; measure actual first-load behavior before changing media architecture.
- The build script has a common `head(title, desc, image)` routine, making it the correct place to implement systematic metadata fixes. Avoid editing 23 generated pages independently without fixing the generator.

### Service pages
- `/filmmaker/services/` has its own H1 and service links, but title `Film services | Bogdan Pascal` could target prospective production clients more precisely.
- `/filmmaker/services/dop/` has H1 and a dedicated cinematography description.
- `/filmmaker/services/fpv/` has H1 and its own FPV/aerial topic.
- `/filmmaker/services/editing/`, `colour-grading/`, `sound-design/` have headings and descriptive metadata.
- `/filmmaker/services/on-set/` is intentionally a `noindex, follow` legacy HTML redirect to `/filmmaker/services/dop/`, with canonical pointing to DoP. Do not index it as a new service page.
- `/filmmaker/equipment/` is intentionally `noindex, nofollow` and currently lacks a meta description. Decide whether it's meant to be a public searchable kit page before changing that.
- `/filmmaker/about/` has H1 `About me.` and description emphasizing DoP/drone; could convey production credentials and your specialized movement background more strongly.
- Staging V2/V3 are separate from the current production pages and have `noindex` in representative checked files. Keep them out of the sitemap and avoid accidentally treating copies as new indexable pages.

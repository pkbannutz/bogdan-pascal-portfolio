## Improved research prompt

Collect competitor search listings for the full set of professional roles represented on bogdanpascal.com, without mistaking adjacent keyword opportunities for verified qualifications. First inspect the existing website and keyword inventory. For each category, choose a commercially relevant UK English Google search query. Capture the first 10 ORGANIC results in their original ranking order, without editorial re-ranking. Record title, exact returned website URL or verified host, date, query, target market, and source. Flag up to 3 clearly labelled paid ads separately if the data source exposes them; never infer ads. Preserve overlapping domains across categories because they reflect separate searches, but avoid duplicates within a single category where possible. Extend searches with synonymous terms, distinctions such as parkour vs freerunning and sound design vs SFX, and separate services such as DoP, gimbal, Ronin, FPV, drone, editing, grading, and sound. If Google ranking positions cannot be verified, label those rows as search-discovery results rather than pretending they are Google's first 10. Do not scrape deeper content or analyze competitors at this stage. Save a flat numbered list grouped by category plus machine-readable CSV for later audit.

## Notes

Search results depend on country, language, device, date, personalized ranking and SERP features. Organic positions do not include sponsored ads, maps, knowledge panels or AI overviews. One Google query per category does not guarantee all synonyms show identical competitors. Where using general web discovery rather than a certified Google SERP provider, rows must be marked accordingly. Separate genuine portfolio and agency sites from directories, educational articles, Wikipedia, social media, job boards and public platforms during *later* research, but don't silently replace true Google ranks at collection time.

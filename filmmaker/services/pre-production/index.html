01 / THE PLAN

Pre production.

I work with the director to turn the brief into a camera plan we can take to set.

Visual development

I work with the director to find the look of the film. We use references to make choices about light, framing and camera movement.

Shot lists and camera planning

I break down the script or brief into shots and plan the lenses, lighting and rigs we’ll need on set.

Location recces

I look at natural light, angles, access and room to move the camera. I also check where aerial shots and action sequences are practical.

Action design and rehearsals

I work out how the performers and camera move together, then rehearse the timing and angles with the team.

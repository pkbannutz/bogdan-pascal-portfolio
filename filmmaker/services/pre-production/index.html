03 / HOW I COLLABORATE

CREATIVE.
PRACTICAL.

WITH THE DIRECTOR

We find the visual rules.

What deserves a close-up? When should the camera move? How can light communicate the shift in a scene? We make the images intentional, not just attractive.

WITH THE PRODUCTION TEAM

We make them possible.

I look at how the ideas translate to locations, available light, lenses, support, drone logistics and the day's schedule. For physical action, performer and camera timing matters too.

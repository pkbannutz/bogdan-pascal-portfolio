THE INTENTION

MORE
THAN A
LOOK.

A change in contrast can alter a mood. A shift in colour can make a moment feel warmer, quieter or more uncertain.

For me, grading starts with the intention of the film. I balance the images, develop the palette and carry that feeling consistently through the edit. The goal is a world that feels considered, not a collection of individually beautiful shots.

03 / THE APPROACH

FROM NEUTRAL
TO NARRATIVE.

The creative grade is only as strong as the foundation underneath it. Each decision has to serve the cut, the people and the story.

01 / BALANCE

GET THE
FOUNDATION
RIGHT.

Exposure, white balance and shot matching come first. Consistent contrast and natural skin tones give the film a base to build on.

02 / LOOK

SHAPE
THE
FEELING.

Develop a palette and tonal character that support the emotional direction. Shape attention through contrast, saturation and colour separation.

03 / CONTINUITY

MAKE IT
ALL
BELONG.

Move through the sequence as a whole. Refine the relationships between shots so the images feel like one film rather than isolated frames.

04 / WORKING TOGETHER

LET'S FIND
THE LOOK.

Whether the intention is natural and understated or something more expressive, the starting point is a conversation about the story.

Tell me what you're making, what you want people to feel and where the finished film will be seen.

HELPFUL FOR A FIRST CONVERSATION
  1. 01
    The project or a private viewing link.
  2. 02
    References, stills or a description of the intended mood.
  3. 03
    The timeline and intended delivery platforms.
  4. 04
    Any technical details about the footage, if known.

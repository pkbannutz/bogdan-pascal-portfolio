A convincing world is built with what you hear.

I work on sound alongside the picture, cleaning dialogue, building atmospheres, adding sound effects and finding a balance that supports the story.

The goal isn't to make everything louder. It's to give the right moments detail and impact, and let the quiet moments breathe.

02 / BUILDING THE WORLD

THE SOUND
HAS LAYERS.

These are the main ingredients I work with, each serving a different part of the scene.

01 / VOICE◉

Dialogue.

Reduce distractions, refine transitions and preserve natural presence so the words feel close and clear.

02 / SPACE◉

Atmosphere.

Room tone, wind, distance and environmental texture give the picture a believable space.

03 / DETAIL◉

Foley & SFX.

Footsteps, movement, cloth, impacts and designed effects give actions weight and character.

04 / FEELING◉

The mix.

Balance speech, ambience, effects and music so important moments reach the audience.

03 / SOUND FOLLOWS STORY

DETAIL WHERE
IT MATTERS.

SUBTLE WORK

Make it feel real.

A clean piece of dialogue, the right distant ambience, or the sound of a body passing close to camera can make a scene feel present without calling attention to itself.

DESIGNED MOMENTS

Give it impact.

Sometimes a movement needs a stronger texture, a shift in perspective needs a change in space, or silence is the most powerful sound choice.

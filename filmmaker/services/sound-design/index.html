04 / HOW I THINK ABOUT SOUND

NOT LOUDER.
MORE ALIVE.

The job is not to fill every second. It is to make the important moments feel present, and to know when silence does more than another effect.

01 / CLEAN
01/

LET THE
VOICE BREATHE.

Reduce distracting noise, shape transitions and keep dialogue clear without stripping away the human quality of the recording.

02 / BUILD
02/

GIVE ACTION
ITS WEIGHT.

Build movement, impact and texture through layered sound effects that feel connected to what happens on screen.

03 / PLACE
03/

MAKE THE
WORLD EXIST.

Shape ambience, distance and space so the world continues beyond the edge of the frame.

04 / FINISH
04/

MAKE IT
ONE STORY.

Balance voice, music and effects into a coherent final mix that supports the cut and the platform it is made for.

About me.

I'm a director of photography and drone operator. Technology keeps me close to the newest tools and techniques in filmmaking.

With more than a decade in parkour and stunts, I plan and execute camera moves, choreograph action and build camera movement into the story from the first frame.

I work with Anda Teglas as BTHEVISUAL, a London based film production duo, on commercials, brand films, music videos and shorts.
